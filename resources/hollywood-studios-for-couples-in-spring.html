Hollywood Studios for Couples in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Spring at Hollywood Studios brings a lively yet manageable atmosphere, making it an ideal season for couples seeking a romantic and thrilling Disney escape. Crowds tend to peak around spring break in mid-March and Easter weekend but ease off in April and May, offering shorter wait times and a more relaxed pace. The warm Florida weather, typically ranging from the mid-70s to mid-80s°F, creates a pleasant backdrop for strolling hand-in-hand through immersive themed lands.

Top attractions for Couples

  • Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance – An epic, story-driven experience that pulls couples into a thrilling shared adventure unlike anything else in the park.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – Work together as a crew to pilot the iconic ship, making it a fun and interactive couples activity.
  • Tower of Terror – A classic adrenaline rush that gives couples a perfect excuse to hold on tight during the suspenseful free-fall drops.
  • Indiana Jones Epic Stunt Spectacular – A dazzling live show that lets couples relax together while enjoying jaw-dropping action and storytelling.
  • Fantasmic! – A breathtaking nighttime spectacular featuring water, fire, and Disney magic, creating an unforgettable romantic end to the evening.

Dining tips

For a truly memorable couples dining experience, book a table at The Hollywood Brown Derby, the park's signature restaurant offering sophisticated dishes and an old-Hollywood ambiance perfect for a romantic meal. Advance dining reservations are essential during spring, especially for popular spots, so secure your table at least 60 days ahead through the Disney website. Couples looking for a casual yet charming option will love sharing shareable bites and specialty cocktails at Oga's Cantina, the Star Wars-themed bar with a one-of-a-kind atmosphere. For a sweet treat, grab a Galaxy's Edge-inspired Blue Milk or a fresh funnel cake to enjoy together as you explore the park.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive early during spring break weeks to ride high-demand attractions like Rise of the Resistance before lines build up significantly.
  • Purchase Lightning Lane passes strategically for your must-do rides, as spring crowds can push wait times beyond 60 minutes for top attractions.
  • Pack light layers and sunscreen, since spring mornings can be mild but afternoons heat up quickly under the Florida sun.
  • Check the park calendar in advance for any special spring events or extended evening hours that could enhance your couples visit.
  • Visit Galaxy's Edge and Toy Story Land later in the evening when families with young children begin to leave, giving couples a quieter and more immersive experience.

How to plan your day

Start your morning by heading straight to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge to experience Rise of the Resistance and Millennium Falcon before crowds build, then work your way through Toy Story Land and the park's thrill rides throughout the afternoon. As the day winds down, enjoy a leisurely dinner at The Hollywood Brown Derby or a drink at Oga's Cantina before securing your spot for the Fantasmic! evening show. Ending the night with Fantasmic! under the stars is the perfect romantic finale to a full and unforgettable Hollywood Studios day together.
