Hollywood Studios for Families with young kids in Winter

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in winter offers families with young kids a magical atmosphere, with festive decorations and holiday overlays adding extra sparkle to the park through December and into the new year. Crowd levels can be high during the holiday season, particularly around Christmas and New Year, but January and February tend to be quieter and more manageable for families pushing strollers or keeping track of little ones. The mild Florida winter weather, typically ranging from the mid-60s to low 70s°F, makes outdoor waiting and exploration far more comfortable than the sweltering summer months.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Toy Story Land: Slinky Dog Dash – This gentle roller coaster is perfectly suited to young children, offering just enough excitement without being overwhelming, set inside the beloved Toy Story universe kids already adore.
  • Alien Swirling Saucers – A classic carnival-style spinning ride themed around Toy Story's iconic claw machine aliens, ideal for toddlers and early school-age children who meet the low height requirement.
  • Disney Junior Dance Party – An interactive live show featuring beloved Disney Junior characters like Mickey Mouse and Doc McStuffins, guaranteed to have young children singing, dancing, and fully engaged.
  • Muppet*Vision 3D – A fun and funny 3D show experience that works beautifully for young kids, offering a cool indoor break while delivering plenty of laughs and gentle surprises.
  • Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (Browsing and Atmosphere) – Even if young children are too small for some rides, the immersive otherworldly environment of Batuu is endlessly fascinating and perfect for imaginative little explorers.

Dining tips

Hollywood Studios has several family-friendly dining options that work well for young children, with Woody's Lunch Box in Toy Story Land being a fan favourite offering simple, kid-approved comfort foods like grilled cheese sandwiches and totchos at affordable quick-service prices. For a sit-down experience, Hollywood and Vine offers a character dining experience where young kids can meet Disney Junior characters while enjoying a buffet-style meal, making it a memorable midday treat worth booking well in advance. During the busy holiday period, mobile ordering through the My Disney Experience app is strongly recommended to avoid long waits with restless toddlers. Be sure to come prepared with snacks for young ones, as wait times between meals at a busy park can stretch a child's patience very thin.

Winter planning tips

  • Book park reservations and dining early – Hollywood Studios is one of Disney World's most in-demand parks, and holiday period slots fill up weeks or even months ahead, so secure your spot as soon as reservations open.
  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park at opening is especially valuable for families with young kids, as it allows you to experience the most popular Toy Story Land attractions before lines build throughout the morning.
  • Pack layers for cooler evenings – Florida winter nights can dip into the low 50s°F, so bring light jackets or hoodies for young children as the temperature drops after sunset during extended park hours.
  • Use the Rider Switch option – For attractions with height requirements that exclude the very youngest children, Disney's Rider Switch programme allows parents to take turns riding without waiting in line twice.
  • Plan for nap time – A midday break back at your resort hotel can make a huge difference for toddlers and preschoolers, allowing them to recharge before returning refreshed for evening entertainment.

How to plan your day

Start your morning by heading straight to Toy Story Land at rope drop to ride Slinky Dog Dash and Alien Swirling Saucers before the queues grow, then work your way through gentler attractions like Muppet*Vision 3D and the Disney Junior Dance Party during the busier midday hours when outdoor waits feel longest. Take a lunch break at Woody's Lunch Box or your pre-booked character dining reservation before considering a resort break if travelling with very young children who need downtime. Return to the park in the late afternoon to explore Galaxy's Edge at a leisurely pace and catch any scheduled evening entertainment, which during the winter season may include special holiday programming that young kids will absolutely love.
