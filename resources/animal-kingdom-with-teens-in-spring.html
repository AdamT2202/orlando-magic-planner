Animal Kingdom for Families with teens in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Animal Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Animal Kingdom with teens in spring offers an exciting mix of thrilling rides, immersive wildlife experiences, and vibrant festival energy across March through May. Crowd levels tend to build from moderate in early March to busier peaks around spring break in late March and April, so planning ahead is essential. Temperatures climb from the mid-70s into the low 90s°F by May, bringing warm sunshine and occasional afternoon thunderstorms that add a dramatic backdrop to the lush, jungle-inspired park atmosphere.

Top attractions for Families with teens

  • Avatar Flight of Passage: This breathtaking simulator ride through Pandora delivers an adrenaline rush that teens consistently rate as a highlight of any Walt Disney World visit.
  • Expedition Everest: A high-speed roller coaster through the Himalayas with a backwards drop that thrills teens who love fast-paced coaster experiences.
  • Kilimanjaro Safaris: An open-air safari truck ride through authentic African savanna landscapes where teens can spot giraffes, lions, and elephants up close.
  • DINOSAUR: An intense dark ride racing through the prehistoric past that balances storytelling with jump scares teens tend to love.
  • Rivers of Light Nighttime Spectacular: A stunning evening water show blending nature, music, and projection effects that makes for a memorable end to the day for the whole family.

Dining tips

Flame Tree Barbecue in Discovery Island is a teen favorite thanks to its generous portions of smoked meats, outdoor riverside seating, and quick-service convenience that keeps pace with an action-packed day. Satu'ali Canteen in Pandora offers globally inspired bowls and plant-based options that adventurous teen eaters will appreciate alongside the otherworldly surroundings. For a sit-down experience, Tiffins Restaurant delivers an upscale menu celebrating world cultures and is ideal for families wanting a relaxed midday break away from the crowds. Be sure to use Disney's mobile ordering feature through the My Disney Experience app to skip long lines and maximize time on attractions during busy spring periods.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive before park opening: Arriving 30 to 45 minutes early during spring break weeks gives your family a head start on securing Lightning Lane passes for Flight of Passage before they sell out.
  • Visit Pandora first thing: Head straight to Pandora – The World of Avatar at rope drop since wait times for Flight of Passage can exceed 90 minutes by mid-morning during spring crowds.
  • Pack for afternoon rain: Spring afternoons in Orlando bring brief but heavy thunderstorms, so carry ponchos and use any downtime in covered areas to grab a snack or browse merchandise.
  • Book dining reservations early: Table service restaurants like Tiffins fill up weeks in advance during spring, so make reservations up to 60 days out through the Disney website.
  • Take advantage of extended evening hours: Disney occasionally offers extended park hours in spring for on-site hotel guests, giving families with teens extra time on popular rides with significantly shorter queues.

How to plan your day

Start your morning in Pandora tackling Avatar Flight of Passage and Na'vi River Journey before crowds build, then work your way through Africa for Kilimanjaro Safaris and on to Asia for Expedition Everest during midday when lines in other areas peak. Use the hottest part of the afternoon for a sit-down lunch, a visit to the shaded Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail, or a midday break back at your resort if you are staying on Disney property. Return refreshed for the evening to catch DinoLand U.S.A., any live entertainment, and close out the night with the Rivers of Light spectacular for a truly complete Animal Kingdom experience with your teens.
