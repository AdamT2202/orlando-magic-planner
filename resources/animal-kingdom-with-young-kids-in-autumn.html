Animal Kingdom for Families with young kids in Autumn

Everything you need to plan the perfect Animal Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Animal Kingdom with young kids during autumn is a wonderful experience, as the summer crowds begin to thin out after Labor Day, making September and October particularly manageable for families with little ones. The Florida heat starts to ease slightly by October and November, with temperatures becoming more comfortable for strolling through the park's lush, expansive grounds. The atmosphere during this season feels immersive and unhurried, with shorter queues and a more relaxed pace that suits families traveling with toddlers and young children.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • The Boneyard – This giant outdoor fossil dig playground in DinoLand U.S.A. is perfect for young kids who love to explore, climb, and burn off energy in a safe, themed environment.
  • Kilimanjaro Safaris – A must-do for animal-loving children, this ride takes families through a real savanna where they can spot giraffes, elephants, and lions up close with no height restrictions.
  • Festival of the Lion King – This spectacular live show features beloved songs, acrobatics, and colorful characters that captivate young audiences and is fully accessible to all ages.
  • Na'vi River Journey – A gentle, visually stunning boat ride through Pandora that enchants younger children with glowing plants, exotic creatures, and a magical shaman animatronic.
  • Wildlife Express Train – A relaxing train journey to Rafiki's Planet Watch where kids can meet animals up close, enjoy petting zoo areas, and visit the conservation station.

Dining tips

Flame Tree Barbecue is a fantastic quick-service option for families, offering hearty portions of ribs, chicken, and sandwiches alongside a shaded outdoor seating area that works well for wriggling toddlers. Restaurantosaurus in DinoLand U.S.A. is a family-friendly spot with familiar comfort foods like burgers and mac and cheese that even the pickiest young eaters tend to enjoy. For a sit-down experience, Tusker House Restaurant offers a character dining breakfast with Donald Duck and friends, which is a magical highlight for young kids and worth booking well in advance. Snack carts throughout the park also make it easy to grab fruit, popcorn, and kid-friendly bites on the go without disrupting your family's stride.

Autumn planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park gates before opening in autumn allows families to experience popular rides like Kilimanjaro Safaris and Na'vi River Journey before any queues build up.
  • Pack layers for the evening – While autumn days remain warm in Orlando, evenings in October and November can feel cooler, so bring a light jacket for young children heading into the night hours.
  • Book dining and character meals early – Even with lower autumn crowds, character dining experiences like Tusker House fill up quickly, so make reservations 60 days in advance through the Disney website.
  • Plan a midday rest break – Even in autumn, Florida afternoons can be warm and tiring for small children, so schedule a break at your resort hotel or a quiet shaded area to avoid meltdowns.
  • Check Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party dates – Running through early November, this separately ticketed evening event at Magic Kingdom pairs beautifully with a daytime Animal Kingdom visit for a memorable full-day family itinerary.

How to plan your day

Start your morning at Animal Kingdom by heading straight to Pandora – The World of Avatar to experience Na'vi River Journey before lines grow, then loop through Africa for the Kilimanjaro Safaris while animals are most active in the cooler morning hours. Spend the middle of the day enjoying the Festival of the Lion King show, exploring The Boneyard playground, and taking the Wildlife Express Train to Rafiki's Planet Watch, pausing for a leisurely lunch at one of the park's family-friendly dining spots. As the afternoon cools down in the autumn season, use the final hours to revisit favourite areas, catch any live entertainment along Discovery Island, and let young kids wind down at a comfortable pace before heading back to your resort.
