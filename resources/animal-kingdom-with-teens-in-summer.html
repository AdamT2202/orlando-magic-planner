Animal Kingdom for Families with teens in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect Animal Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Animal Kingdom with teens in summer means immersing your family in one of Disney's most visually stunning parks during its busiest and most energetic season. Expect large crowds, particularly from late June through mid-August, with wait times for top attractions regularly exceeding 60 to 90 minutes. The Florida heat and humidity are intense, with temperatures frequently reaching the mid-90s Fahrenheit, but the park's lush vegetation, shaded trails, and thrilling experiences make it an unforgettable summer destination for older kids and their parents.

Top attractions for Families with teens

  • Avatar Flight of Passage – This breathtaking 3D simulator ride through Pandora is widely considered one of the best theme park experiences in the world, delivering the kind of adrenaline rush that teens crave.
  • Expedition Everest – A fast, twisting roller coaster through a haunted Himalayan mountain that offers enough speed and suspense to keep teens engaged and excited for repeat rides.
  • Kilimanjaro Safaris – A guided open-vehicle safari through a realistic African savanna gives teens a genuine wildlife encounter with giraffes, elephants, and lions that feels nothing like a typical theme park ride.
  • Rivers of Light and evening shows – The park's nighttime entertainment features stunning water projections and live performances that offer a more relaxed but visually spectacular wind-down experience for the whole family.
  • DINOSAUR – This intense dark ride sends families on a high-energy mission back to the Cretaceous period, with enough jump scares and motion to keep thrill-seeking teens thoroughly entertained.

Dining tips

Animal Kingdom offers a strong variety of dining options that suit the appetites and adventurous tastes of teenagers, from quick-service spots to immersive table-service restaurants. Satu'li Canteen in Pandora is a must-visit for teens, offering customizable bowls with bold flavors in a visually stunning Pandora-themed setting that feels genuinely unique. Flame Tree Barbecue is a popular quick-service choice with generous portions of smoked meats that are ideal for hungry teens after a morning of riding. For a more elevated experience, consider booking a reservation at Tiffins, Animal Kingdom's signature fine-dining restaurant, where globally inspired dishes and an artful atmosphere make for a memorable mid-day break from the heat and crowds.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive before park opening – Getting to Animal Kingdom at least 30 minutes before the official opening time allows your family to reach the highest-demand rides like Avatar Flight of Passage before wait times explode past an hour.
  • Use Disney Lightning Lane strategically – Purchase Lightning Lane passes for Flight of Passage and Expedition Everest early in the day, as these sell out quickly during peak summer weeks and can save hours of waiting.
  • Plan midday breaks – Schedule a sit-down meal, an indoor show, or a return to your resort hotel between noon and 3 p.m. when the heat is at its most intense and crowds at the most congested.
  • Stay for extended evening hours – Summer often brings extended park hours for Disney resort guests, and attending in the evening means shorter queues, cooler temperatures, and a magical atmosphere with illuminated Pandora glowing at night.
  • Pack smart for the weather – Bring lightweight clothing, reef-safe sunscreen, refillable water bottles, and a compact poncho for the frequent afternoon thunderstorms that roll through Orlando almost daily in summer.

How to plan your day

Start your day in Pandora first thing in the morning, targeting Avatar Flight of Passage and Na'vi River Journey before the queues build up, then move on to Expedition Everest and DINOSAUR in the mid-morning while energy and patience are still high. Use the hottest part of the afternoon to enjoy shaded experiences like the Festival of the Lion King, the Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail, or a long relaxed lunch at one of the park's dining destinations. Return to the most popular rides in the early evening when crowds thin out, and finish the night in Pandora to witness the bioluminescent landscape after dark, which is an absolute highlight for teens visiting Animal Kingdom in summer.
