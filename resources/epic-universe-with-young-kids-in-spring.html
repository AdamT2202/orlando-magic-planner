Epic Universe for Families with young kids in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Epic Universe trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Epic Universe with young kids in spring means stepping into a world of wonder during one of Orlando's most vibrant seasons, with blooming landscapes and comfortably warm temperatures typically ranging from the mid-60s to low 80s°F. Spring break periods in March and April bring noticeably higher crowd levels, particularly around Easter and school holiday weeks, so expect busier queues at family-friendly attractions. The atmosphere throughout the park during spring is energetic and festive, with seasonal decorations and special entertainment that young children will absolutely love.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Celestial Park's interactive fountain area – A perfect sensory experience for toddlers and young children who love to splash and explore without the intensity of thrill rides.
  • The Wizarding World of Harry Potter – Ministry of Magic – Immersive theming and gentler family rides make this world magical for curious young imaginations discovering the franchise for the first time.
  • How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk – Featuring family-friendly rides and beloved characters that young kids recognise, this land is perfectly scaled for smaller adventurers.
  • Super Nintendo World – Bright, colourful environments and interactive Power-Up Band activities keep young children engaged, excited, and entertained throughout the entire land.
  • Character meet-and-greets across the park – Young kids will treasure face-to-face moments with favourite characters, and spring lighting makes for beautiful family photos.

Dining tips

Epic Universe offers a wide variety of family-friendly dining options that cater specifically to young children, including kid's menus with familiar favourites like pasta, chicken tenders, and fruit at multiple table-service and quick-service locations throughout the park. Booking dining reservations well in advance is strongly recommended during the spring season, especially over spring break weeks when popular restaurants fill up quickly. Consider visiting themed quick-service locations during off-peak lunch hours, around 11am or after 2pm, to avoid long waits with restless little ones. Many dining locations also offer allergy-friendly menu options, so be sure to notify staff of any dietary requirements when ordering or when making reservations.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive at park opening – Young children are typically at their most energetic in the morning, and arriving early lets families ride top attractions before crowds build significantly.
  • Check the school holiday calendar – Avoid peak spring break weeks if possible, as crowd levels spike dramatically during these periods, making the experience more stressful with young kids.
  • Pack sun protection and rain gear – Spring in Orlando brings unpredictable afternoon showers and strong sunshine, so sunscreen, hats, and a compact poncho are essential items for families.
  • Use the Universal app for wait times – Monitor live queue times through the official app to redirect your family efficiently and avoid wasting precious nap-window time standing in long lines.
  • Build in rest breaks – Schedule quieter mid-afternoon downtime at a shaded seating area or return to your hotel for a nap, allowing young children to recharge for a magical evening experience.

How to plan your day

Start your day at Epic Universe by heading directly to your young child's most anticipated land as soon as the park opens, tackling the highest-demand family attractions before mid-morning crowds arrive in full force. Structure your afternoon around dining reservations, shorter wait-time attractions, and interactive play areas, allowing flexibility for the spontaneous moments that make theme park days truly memorable for little ones. As the day winds down and evening entertainment begins, the spring twilight atmosphere creates a breathtaking backdrop, giving young families the perfect opportunity to experience parades, nighttime shows, or a final character greeting before heading home.
