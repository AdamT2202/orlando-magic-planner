Spring · March–May

Epic Universe for Couples in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Epic Universe trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Spring is one of the most romantic and visually stunning times to visit Epic Universe in Orlando, with blooming landscapes and warm but manageable temperatures ranging from the mid-60s to the low-80s Fahrenheit. Crowds tend to pick up during spring break weeks in March and April, but early May offers a sweet spot of lighter attendance and a relaxed, intimate atmosphere perfect for couples. The parks come alive with seasonal energy, making it an ideal backdrop for shared adventures and unforgettable memories together.

Top attractions for Couples

  • Ministry of Magic: Step into the wizarding world together and share the magic of immersive spell-casting experiences and enchanting environments perfect for couples who love fantasy.
  • Celestial Park: A beautifully designed central hub with breathtaking cosmic theming, ideal for leisurely strolls, photo opportunities, and romantic moments between attractions.
  • The Monsters Unchained: A thrilling dark ride that delivers shared screams and adrenaline rushes, bringing couples closer through heart-pounding excitement.
  • How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk: Immerse yourselves in a whimsical, story-rich world with gentle rides and stunning scenery that appeal to couples seeking wonder over intensity.
  • Universal Epic's signature evening shows: Live entertainment and spectacular nighttime productions create naturally romantic settings for couples to enjoy side by side.

Dining tips

Epic Universe offers a wide range of dining experiences tailored to couples looking to extend their adventure beyond the rides, from immersive themed restaurants to scenic outdoor cafes perfect for a relaxed midday break. For a special experience, consider booking a table-service reservation in advance at one of the park's signature themed dining venues, where the atmosphere is as memorable as the food. Sharing a themed cocktail or butterbeer-style beverage while soaking in your surroundings is a quintessential couples moment at Epic Universe. Arriving early for dining reservations during spring break periods is strongly advised, as popular restaurants fill up quickly.

Spring planning tips

  • Visit during early May: Avoid the peak spring break rush by planning your trip in early to mid-May, when crowds thin out and wait times are significantly shorter.
  • Arrive at park opening: Heading straight to the most popular attractions first thing in the morning allows couples to experience headline rides before queues build throughout the day.
  • Book dining and experiences in advance: Spring demand is high, so reserving table-service restaurants and any special couple-friendly experiences weeks ahead is essential.
  • Pack for variable weather: Spring mornings can be cool while afternoons turn warm and humid, so layering and carrying a compact rain poncho will keep you comfortable all day.
  • Take advantage of Universal's early park admission: Staying at an on-site Universal hotel grants couples early access to the park, a major advantage during busy spring periods.

How to plan your day

Couples should begin their Epic Universe day by targeting the most immersive and high-demand worlds first thing in the morning, such as Ministry of Magic or the Monsters universe, before transitioning to more leisurely experiences like Celestial Park as the day progresses. Midday is ideal for a sit-down dining reservation, allowing you to rest, reconnect, and recharge away from the afternoon heat before diving back into the remaining worlds together. Close your day in the evening with the park's nighttime entertainment and ambient lighting, which transforms Epic Universe into one of the most romantic settings in all of Orlando.
