Animal Kingdom for Families with teens in Winter

Everything you need to plan the perfect Animal Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Animal Kingdom with teens in winter offers a refreshing escape from the summer heat, with temperatures typically ranging from the mid-50s to low 70s Fahrenheit, making it far more comfortable for long days on your feet. Crowd levels are generally lower in January and February, though the holiday period around Christmas and New Year sees a significant surge in visitors. The park takes on a festive atmosphere in December, with seasonal decorations and special entertainment that teens and parents alike will appreciate.

Top attractions for Families with teens

  • Avatar Flight of Passage – This breathtaking ride through Pandora is a must for teens, delivering a thrilling and visually stunning experience that consistently ranks as one of Walt Disney World's best attractions.
  • Expedition Everest – A fast-paced roller coaster through a haunted Himalayan mountain that delivers enough excitement to satisfy thrill-seeking teenagers without being overwhelming.
  • DINOSAUR – An intense time-travel adventure that combines storytelling with unexpected drops and scares, making it a hit with teens who enjoy a good adrenaline rush.
  • Kilimanjaro Safaris – An immersive open-air safari experience where teens can spot real African animals up close, with cooler winter temperatures making wildlife more active and visible.
  • Rivers of Light – An evening spectacular combining live animals, water screens, and stunning projections that provides a memorable and visually impressive close to the day.

Dining tips

Flame Tree Barbecue is a popular quick-service option that satisfies hungry teens with generous portions of smoked meats and sides at reasonable prices, and the outdoor seating area overlooks a scenic waterway. For a more immersive sit-down experience, Tiffins Restaurant offers globally inspired cuisine that adventurous teen palates will enjoy, and the sophisticated atmosphere makes it feel like a special occasion. Satu'li Canteen in Pandora is a fantastic choice for families who want a unique quick-service meal featuring build-your-own bowls with flavorful, wholesome ingredients. Be sure to make advance dining reservations for table-service restaurants before your visit, as availability fills up quickly even during the quieter winter months.

Winter planning tips

  • Arrive early during the holiday period – Christmas through New Year is one of the busiest times at Walt Disney World, so arriving at rope drop gives your family the best chance to experience top attractions before queues build.
  • Layer your clothing – Winter mornings and evenings can be surprisingly chilly, so pack light jackets or hoodies that can be tied around your waist during the warmer midday hours.
  • Book Lightning Lane passes strategically – Use Disney's Lightning Lane Multi Pass for high-demand rides like Expedition Everest and secure an Individual Lightning Lane for Avatar Flight of Passage as soon as the park opens.
  • Check for extended park hours – Disney occasionally offers extended evening hours for select guests during winter, giving families additional time to enjoy attractions with reduced crowds.
  • Take advantage of animal sightings in cooler weather – Animals are far more active during the cooler winter months, so plan your Kilimanjaro Safaris ride earlier in the day when activity levels are at their peak.

How to plan your day

Start your day at rope drop by heading straight to Pandora – The World of Avatar to ride Avatar Flight of Passage before the queue reaches its notoriously long peak wait times, then work your way to Expedition Everest and DINOSAUR while energy is high and crowds are still manageable. Use the midday hours to explore Discovery Island, enjoy lunch at one of the park's quick-service restaurants, and browse Harambe Market in Africa, reserving any Lightning Lane passes for early afternoon when standby lines tend to peak. Wrap up your evening with the Kilimanjaro Safaris for a quieter late-day experience and position your family for any nighttime entertainment, ensuring your teens leave with memories that will last well beyond the winter break.
