Animal Kingdom for Families with young kids in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Animal Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Animal Kingdom with young kids in spring offers a magical blend of lush greenery, blooming landscapes, and vibrant animal activity that makes the park feel especially alive. Crowd levels tend to build through spring break in late March and April, so arriving early is essential, though mid-May offers a welcome quieter window before summer rushes in. The warm, sunny weather creates a fantastic outdoor atmosphere, though afternoon showers are common, adding a refreshing break to your family adventure.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Kilimanjaro Safaris: A must-do for little ones who light up at real giraffes, elephants, and lions roaming freely across the savanna landscape.
  • The Boneyard: A dedicated dig-site play area where young kids can burn energy climbing, sliding, and exploring in a shaded, safe environment.
  • It's Tough to Be a Bug!: A fun 4D show inside the Tree of Life that delights younger children with its silly sensory surprises and colourful characters.
  • Tricera Top Spin: A gentle spinning ride in DinoLand U.S.A. perfectly sized and paced for toddlers and preschoolers seeking their first theme park thrill.
  • Festival of the Lion King: An energetic, colourful live stage show that captivates young kids with beloved songs, acrobatics, and interactive audience participation.

Dining tips

Flame Tree Barbecue is an excellent quick-service choice for families, offering kid-friendly proteins and a beautiful outdoor seating area overlooking the Discovery River. Restaurantosaurus in DinoLand U.S.A. serves classic American comfort food like burgers and chicken nuggets that picky young eaters tend to love. For a sit-down experience, Tusker House offers a popular character dining breakfast with Donald Duck and friends, which is worth booking well in advance during busy spring periods. Bringing your own snacks and a refillable water bottle can also help keep little ones fuelled between meals without derailing the day.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop: Getting to the park gates 30 minutes before opening allows young families to tackle top attractions before spring break crowds swell mid-morning.
  • Pack rain gear: Spring afternoons in Orlando frequently bring short, heavy showers, so lightweight ponchos for every family member will keep the day moving without soggy meltdowns.
  • Schedule a midday rest: Return to your hotel between noon and 3pm during the hottest, most crowded part of the day, then head back refreshed for evening entertainment.
  • Book dining and Lightning Lane early: Spring break weeks are among the busiest of the year, so secure restaurant reservations and priority ride access 60 days in advance.
  • Apply sunscreen frequently: The Florida spring sun is stronger than many visitors expect, so reapply SPF protection every two hours, especially after water play areas.

How to plan your day

Start your morning with Kilimanjaro Safaris and Expedition Everest before heading to Pandora – The World of Avatar, where Na'vi River Journey offers a gentle, stunning experience ideal for young children. Use the quieter midday hours for The Boneyard, character meet-and-greets, and a relaxed lunch, saving the Festival of the Lion King and Tricera Top Spin for the cooler, slightly less crowded late afternoon. Ending the evening near the Discovery River Amphitheater for the Rivers of Light-style entertainment creates a memorable, low-stress finale perfectly suited to young families.
