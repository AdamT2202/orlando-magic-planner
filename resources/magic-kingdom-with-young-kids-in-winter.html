Magic Kingdom for Families with young kids in Winter

Everything you need to plan the perfect Magic Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Magic Kingdom with young kids during winter brings a truly enchanting experience, as the park is transformed with festive holiday decorations, seasonal entertainment, and a magical atmosphere that little ones will absolutely love. Crowd levels can be moderate to high around the Christmas and New Year holiday period, but January and February tend to be significantly quieter, making it easier to enjoy attractions without long waits. The weather in Orlando during winter is mild and comfortable, with temperatures typically ranging from the mid-50s to low 70s Fahrenheit, making it ideal for families who want to avoid the intense summer heat.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Dumbo the Flying Elephant – A classic gentle ride that thrills toddlers and young children with its slow, soaring circular motion and colorful circus theming.
  • Seven Dwarfs Mine Train – A mildly exciting roller coaster that is perfectly sized for young kids ready for their first thrill ride, featuring beloved characters from Snow White.
  • it's a small world – A slow, calming boat ride filled with singing dolls and bright colors that captivates even the youngest visitors and offers a welcome rest for parents.
  • The Many Adventures of Winnie the Pooh – A charming ride through the Hundred Acre Wood that delights toddlers with familiar characters and gentle surprises at every turn.
  • Mickey's PhilharMagic – A 4D theater show featuring beloved Disney characters and music that keeps young children engaged and entertained while giving tired feet a well-earned break.

Dining tips

Magic Kingdom offers a wide variety of family-friendly dining options that cater well to the tastes and pace of young children. For a truly memorable meal, book a character dining experience at The Crystal Palace, where Winnie the Pooh and friends visit your table, giving little ones an unforgettable up-close character interaction without standing in a separate meet-and-greet line. Quick-service spots like Pinocchio Village Haus and Cosmic Ray's Starlight Café offer kid-friendly menu options such as pasta, pizza, and chicken strips, making mealtimes easy and stress-free. Be sure to make dining reservations well in advance, especially for the holiday season, as popular restaurants fill up quickly during the winter months.

Winter planning tips

  • Arrive early on holiday weeks – During December and the days surrounding Christmas and New Year, arrive at the park at least 30 minutes before opening to make the most of shorter morning queues.
  • Pack layers for cooler evenings – Winter nights in Orlando can drop into the 50s, so bring light jackets or hoodies for young children to stay comfortable during evening fireworks and parades.
  • Take advantage of Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party – If visiting in early December, consider purchasing tickets to this separate ticketed event for exclusive entertainment, character meet-and-greets, and complimentary cookies and cocoa.
  • Use the Disney Genie+ service – Booking Lightning Lane selections through Genie+ helps families with young kids minimize wait times and spend more energy on experiences rather than standing in queues.
  • Plan for an afternoon rest break – Young children tire quickly, so consider returning to your hotel for a nap mid-afternoon and heading back to the park refreshed for the evening entertainment and holiday fireworks.

How to plan your day

Start your morning at Magic Kingdom by heading straight to Fantasyland as soon as the park opens, tackling Seven Dwarfs Mine Train and Dumbo the Flying Elephant before crowds build up throughout the day. In the late morning and early afternoon, work your way through gentler attractions like it's a small world and Mickey's PhilharMagic, pausing for a sit-down lunch at a character dining restaurant to keep young ones energized and excited. Return to the park in the early evening to enjoy the reduced winter crowds in January or February, and position your family on Main Street U.S.A. well ahead of the nightly fireworks spectacular for an unforgettable end to a magical day.
