SeaWorld Orlando for Families with young kids in Winter

Everything you need to plan the perfect SeaWorld Orlando trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting SeaWorld Orlando with young kids during winter (December through February) offers a refreshingly manageable experience compared to the busy summer months, with noticeably shorter wait times and less crowding across most of the park. Temperatures typically range from the mid-50s to low 70s Fahrenheit, making it comfortable for little ones to explore without the sweltering heat of Florida summers. The park comes alive with festive holiday energy in December thanks to the Christmas Celebration event, complete with seasonal shows, decorations, and character meet-and-greets that young children absolutely love.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Sesame Street Land: This colourful, immersive area is perfectly designed for toddlers and young children, featuring gentle rides like Elmo's Choo Choo Train and character meet-and-greets with beloved Sesame Street friends.
  • Dolphin Nursery: Young kids are fascinated watching playful dolphins up close at this viewing area, making it both entertaining and quietly educational without any scary elements.
  • Antarctica: Empire of the Penguin: The gentle family ride version of this attraction gives little ones a fun, immersive adventure through icy penguin habitats that captivates young imaginations.
  • Stingray Lagoon: Kids absolutely delight in the chance to touch and feed stingrays at this shallow, accessible feeding pool, offering a memorable hands-on wildlife encounter.
  • Sea of Shallows: This walkthrough exhibit featuring sharks, rays, and sea turtles is exciting for young children without any height requirements or overwhelming sensory elements.

Dining tips

SeaWorld Orlando offers several family-friendly dining options well suited to picky young eaters, including familiar favourites like chicken tenders, mac and cheese, and pizza available at quick-service locations throughout the park. The Waterway Grill and Sharks Underwater Grill provide sit-down experiences where parents can relax while kids enjoy themed surroundings, though reservations for the latter are strongly recommended even in quieter winter months. All-Day Dining Deals are worth considering for families with young children, as unpredictable appetites and snack-heavy eating habits make unlimited meal plans particularly cost-effective. Be sure to bring any allergy information clearly noted, as SeaWorld staff are generally well-trained in accommodating common food allergies for young guests.

Winter planning tips

  • Layer clothing for young children: Winter mornings at SeaWorld can feel genuinely chilly, especially near water exhibits, so dress kids in removable layers they can shed as temperatures warm through the afternoon.
  • Arrive early on weekends: While winter crowds are lighter overall, weekends in December see significant increases due to holiday visitors and the Christmas Celebration event, so aim to arrive at park opening.
  • Book holiday show times in advance: Seasonal performances during the Christmas Celebration fill up quickly with families, so check the daily schedule online before your visit and plan seating around nap times.
  • Take advantage of reduced wait times on weekdays: January and February weekdays are among the quietest periods in the park's entire year, making them ideal for young families wanting to experience multiple attractions without exhausting queues.
  • Pack a stroller or rent one on arrival: The park covers significant ground, and young children will tire quickly, especially in cooler weather when they are less inclined to walk long distances between exhibits.

How to plan your day

Begin your morning at Sesame Street Land as soon as the park opens, allowing young children to enjoy the rides and meet their favourite characters before any crowds build up later in the day. Move through the animal exhibits and interactive experiences like Stingray Lagoon and the Dolphin Nursery in the mid-morning, scheduling lunch before the early afternoon so younger kids avoid long waits when hunger and tiredness peak simultaneously. Wrap up the day with any remaining shows or a gentle return visit to favourite attractions, keeping in mind that winter days are shorter and park closing times may be earlier than during summer months.
