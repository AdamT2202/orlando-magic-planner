Islands of Adventure for Families with young kids in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect Islands of Adventure trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Islands of Adventure in summer is a vibrant, exciting destination for families with young kids, though be prepared for large crowds as June through August marks peak tourist season. The Florida heat and humidity are intense, with temperatures regularly climbing above 90°F, making water-based attractions especially popular and refreshing. Despite the busy atmosphere, the park's themed lands create a magical, immersive experience that young children will absolutely love.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • The Cat in the Hat: This gentle, family-friendly dark ride through beloved Dr. Seuss stories is perfectly paced for toddlers and young children who enjoy colorful, imaginative storytelling.
  • Caro-Seuss-el: A beautifully themed carousel featuring unique Seuss characters instead of traditional horses, ideal for little ones who love a classic spinning ride without any scary elements.
  • Jurassic World Discovery Center: Young dinosaur fans will be thrilled by the interactive exhibits, life-size dinosaur replicas, and hands-on activities that make learning exciting and engaging.
  • Popeye and Bluto's Bilge-Rat Barges: A fun, splashy river raft ride that provides welcome relief from the summer heat while offering enough excitement to delight young adventurers.
  • Me Ship, The Olive: This interactive play area filled with water cannons, climbing structures, and slides gives young kids a chance to burn energy in a safe, enclosed environment.

Dining tips

Families with young kids will find plenty of kid-friendly dining options scattered throughout Islands of Adventure, with quick-service restaurants offering familiar favorites like burgers, chicken tenders, and macaroni and cheese. The Green Eggs and Ham Café in Seuss Landing is a must-visit for families, serving playfully colored dishes that delight young imaginations and make mealtime part of the adventure. To avoid the intense summer heat, try scheduling your main meal during midday inside an air-conditioned restaurant, giving everyone a much-needed cool-down break. Many dining locations offer kids' meals with reasonable portions and prices, and most can accommodate common dietary needs if you ask a team member in advance.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop: Getting to the park the moment it opens allows families with young kids to experience the most popular attractions before lines grow excessively long in the midday heat.
  • Pack a change of clothes: Water rides like Popeye and Bluto's Bilge-Rat Barges will leave everyone soaked, so bringing a spare outfit for each child prevents uncomfortable hours in wet clothing.
  • Schedule a midday break: Plan to return to your hotel between noon and 3 PM when heat and crowds peak, then head back to the park in the cooler late afternoon and evening hours.
  • Apply sunscreen frequently: Florida summer sun is fierce, so bring high-SPF sunscreen and reapply every two hours, especially after water rides or splash areas where children are frequently wet.
  • Download the Universal app: Use the official app to check real-time wait times, locate character meet-and-greets, and find the nearest first aid or baby care facilities quickly when you need them.

How to plan your day

Start your day in Seuss Landing first thing in the morning, as the colorful, low-thrill attractions are perfect for warming up young kids and the shorter early-morning lines make for a stress-free experience. Move through the Lost Continent and Jurassic World during mid-morning before retreating to your hotel for a midday rest during the hottest, most crowded hours of the day. Return to the park in the late afternoon to enjoy water rides, evening entertainment, and a final loop through any favorite attractions your little ones want to experience one more time before heading home.
