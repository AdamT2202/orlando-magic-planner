Islands of Adventure for Couples in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Islands of Adventure trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Spring brings a lively yet manageable energy to Islands of Adventure, making it an ideal season for couples seeking a mix of thrills and shared memories. March through May sees warm, sunny weather with temperatures climbing from the mid-70s into the mid-80s°F, perfect for exploring the park's immersive themed lands together. Crowds are moderate, peaking around spring break weeks in late March and early April, but shoulder weeks offer shorter wait times and a more relaxed romantic atmosphere.

Top attractions for Couples

  • Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure – Share a side-by-side motorbike and sidecar ride through the Wizarding World, making it one of the most intimate and breathtaking experiences in the park.
  • Jurassic World VelociCoaster – Get your adrenaline racing together on this high-speed coaster, perfect for couples who love a shared thrill.
  • The Amazing Adventures of Spider-Man – A classic interactive dark ride that blends humor and action, ideal for couples who enjoy a slightly gentler but still exciting adventure.
  • Skull Island: Reign of Kong – Immerse yourselves in a dramatic, story-driven experience that sparks conversation and excitement long after the ride ends.
  • Hogsmeade Village stroll – Walk hand-in-hand through the enchanting Harry Potter-themed streets, sipping Butterbeer and soaking in the magical atmosphere together.

Dining tips

For couples looking to share a memorable meal, the Three Broomsticks in Hogsmeade offers a charming, atmospheric setting with hearty dishes like rotisserie chicken and Great Feast platters ideal for sharing. Mythos Restaurant, nestled inside a dramatic cave-like structure near the Forbidden Journey, is widely regarded as one of the best theme park restaurants in the world and is perfect for a relaxed sit-down lunch away from the crowds. Booking a table at Mythos in advance through the Universal app is strongly recommended during spring, especially around peak spring break weeks. For a lighter bite, couples can graze on themed snacks throughout the park, from Butterbeer ice cream to Cauldron Cakes, turning the dining experience itself into a fun shared adventure.

Spring planning tips

  • Avoid spring break peak weeks (typically the last two weeks of March and first week of April) if possible, as crowds surge significantly and wait times can double.
  • Arrive at park opening to tackle the most popular rides like Hagrid's and VelociCoaster before mid-morning queues build up.
  • Purchase Universal Express Passes to maximize your time together on attractions rather than spending precious hours waiting in line.
  • Pack lightweight layers, sunscreen, and a compact umbrella, as spring afternoons can bring brief Florida showers that pass quickly.
  • Download the Universal Orlando app before your visit to check live wait times, make dining reservations, and plan a route that keeps backtracking to a minimum.

How to plan your day

Start your morning at the Wizarding World of Harry Potter – Hogsmeade to experience Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure and Hogsmeade Village before the crowds arrive, giving you the most magical and unhurried experience of the day. Spend the midday hours exploring Marvel Super Hero Island and Jurassic World, taking advantage of shorter queues and grabbing a leisurely lunch at Mythos Restaurant. In the late afternoon, slow the pace and wander through Port of Entry and the Lost Continent, enjoying the architecture, street performers, and spring evening light before wrapping up your day with a final Butterbeer toast in Hogsmeade.
