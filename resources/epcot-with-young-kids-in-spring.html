EPCOT for Families with young kids in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect EPCOT trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting EPCOT with young children in spring means enjoying mild, comfortable temperatures ranging from the mid-60s to low 80s°F, making it ideal for exploring both Future World and the World Showcase. Crowd levels tend to rise during spring break weeks in March and April, but early May typically sees a welcome drop in visitor numbers. The park buzzes with color and energy thanks to the EPCOT International Flower and Garden Festival, which runs throughout spring and delights little ones with its whimsical topiaries shaped like beloved Disney characters.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Frozen Ever After – This gentle boat ride through the world of Arendelle is perfectly paced for young children and features familiar characters that toddlers and preschoolers absolutely love.
  • Remy's Ratatouille Adventure – A family-friendly trackless ride that shrinks guests to the size of a rat, offering fun and excitement without being too intense for little ones.
  • Disney and Pixar Short Film Festival – An air-conditioned 4D cinema experience that gives young kids a fun, sensory-friendly break from walking around the park.
  • Living with the Land – A calm and educational boat ride through real working greenhouses that sparks curiosity in young minds about plants and food.
  • Flower and Garden Festival Topiaries – Strolling past giant character topiaries is free with park admission and creates magical photo moments that children genuinely get excited about.

Dining tips

EPCOT offers an impressive range of dining options, and families with young children will find quick-service locations like Sunshine Seasons in The Land pavilion ideal for picky eaters, with a broad menu that includes kid-friendly staples. The Flower and Garden Festival's outdoor kitchens are scattered across the park and offer small-portion dishes that make it easy to snack your way around the World Showcase without committing to a sit-down meal. For a special experience, Garden Grill Restaurant offers a rotating farm-to-table menu alongside character meet-and-greets with Mickey Mouse and friends, which young children find absolutely thrilling. Be sure to make dining reservations at least 60 days in advance, as popular table-service restaurants fill up quickly during the busy spring season.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park when it opens allows families to ride the most popular attractions like Frozen Ever After and Remy's Ratatouille Adventure before lines build up significantly.
  • Book during non-peak spring break weeks – Crowds are noticeably lighter in early March and early May, so if your schedule allows, avoid the peak spring break window of mid-March through mid-April.
  • Pack sun protection and layers – Spring weather in Orlando can shift quickly, so bring sunscreen, hats, and a light rain jacket to handle afternoon showers common during this season.
  • Use Lightning Lane strategically – Purchase Lightning Lane selections for the highest-demand rides early in the morning to minimize wait times and keep young children from getting overtired standing in queues.
  • Plan a midday rest break – Young children benefit greatly from a break during the hottest part of the day; consider returning to your hotel for a nap and heading back to the park in the cooler late afternoon.

How to plan your day

Start your morning in World Discovery and World Nature, tackling top rides like Frozen Ever After and Living with the Land as early as possible before crowds swell throughout the day. After a midday break or a relaxed lunch, return to the park in the late afternoon to explore the World Showcase countries, letting young children engage with the character topiaries and cultural entertainment along the way. Wrap up your evening near the World Showcase lagoon for EPCOT's nighttime spectacular, which provides a dazzling and memorable finale that even the youngest visitors will talk about long after the trip is over.
