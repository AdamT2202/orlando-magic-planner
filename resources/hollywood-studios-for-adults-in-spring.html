Hollywood Studios for Adults-only groups in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in spring offers adult groups an exciting mix of immersive experiences and iconic entertainment, with the park buzzing with energy as spring break crowds peak in March before tapering off in April and May. Temperatures climb comfortably into the mid-70s to low-80s Fahrenheit, making it warm enough to enjoy outdoor areas like Galaxy's Edge without the oppressive summer heat. The atmosphere during spring is festive and lively, with seasonal entertainment adding extra flair to an already dynamic park.

Top attractions for Adults-only groups

  • Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance – An immersive, show-stopping ride that delivers a cinematic experience unlike anything else in theme parks, perfect for groups who want to be fully blown away.
  • Tower of Terror – A classic thrill ride with a sophisticated, story-driven atmosphere that adults consistently rate as one of Disney's best, combining suspense with genuine scares.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – An interactive, role-based ride where adult groups can take on pilot, gunner, or engineer roles, making it uniquely competitive and social.
  • Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring Aerosmith – A high-speed indoor coaster with inversions and a loud rock soundtrack that appeals directly to thrill-seeking adult groups.
  • Indiana Jones Epic Stunt Spectacular – A jaw-dropping live stunt show that offers adults a cinematic spectacle with behind-the-scenes insight, ideal for a mid-day break between rides.

Dining tips

Adult groups should prioritize a table-service reservation at Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant, where you dine in retro-styled cars while watching classic B-movie clips, creating a uniquely nostalgic and social atmosphere. The Hollywood Brown Derby is the park's premium dining option, offering sophisticated cocktails and upscale American cuisine that suits groups looking for a more refined mid-visit meal. For casual grazing, Oga's Cantina in Galaxy's Edge is an absolute must-visit for adults, serving creative, Star Wars-themed alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages in an extraordinarily immersive bar setting. Be sure to book reservations for table-service spots well in advance through the My Disney Experience app, as spring crowds mean availability disappears quickly.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive before park opening – Early entry gives adult groups first access to Rise of the Resistance and Tower of Terror before wait times climb significantly as the day progresses.
  • Purchase Lightning Lane passes strategically – Use Individual Lightning Lane for Rise of the Resistance and the Multi Pass for secondary attractions to maximize the number of rides your group completes.
  • Visit during weekdays in late April or May – Spring break rushes subside significantly after mid-April, offering noticeably shorter queues and a more relaxed experience for adult groups.
  • Check the entertainment schedule in advance – Fantasmic! and other live shows have limited showtimes and fill up fast in spring, so plan your evening around confirmed performance times.
  • Dress in breathable layers – Spring mornings can be cool but afternoons warm up quickly, so light, moisture-wicking clothing keeps the group comfortable throughout a full park day.

How to plan your day

Adult groups should begin their Hollywood Studios day by heading straight to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at rope drop to experience Rise of the Resistance and Millennium Falcon before lines build, then work through Sunset Boulevard's thrill rides mid-morning while crowds shift toward the newer lands. Schedule a sit-down lunch or a stop at Oga's Cantina in the early afternoon as a social recharge, then use Lightning Lane passes to revisit top attractions before positioning your group for an evening performance of Fantasmic! to close out the day on a spectacular high note.
