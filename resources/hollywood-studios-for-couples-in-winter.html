Hollywood Studios for Couples in Winter

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in winter offers couples a magical atmosphere filled with festive holiday overlays, cooler temperatures, and a more romantic ambiance than the sweltering summer months. Crowd levels can spike around Christmas and New Year, but January and February tend to bring lighter attendance, making it easier to experience top attractions without lengthy waits. The cool, comfortable weather ranging from the mid-50s to low 70s°F creates ideal conditions for leisurely strolling through Sunset Boulevard and Galaxy's Edge hand in hand.

Top attractions for Couples

  • Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance – An immersive, jaw-dropping adventure that gives couples a shared epic experience unlike anything else in the park.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – A fun, interactive ride where couples can team up and take on complementary roles together as co-pilots or crew.
  • Tower of Terror – A thrilling drop tower that delivers adrenaline-fueled excitement and the perfect excuse to hold on tight to your partner.
  • Fantasmic! Nighttime Spectacular – A breathtaking outdoor show combining fireworks, water effects, and beloved characters, ideal for a romantic evening finale.
  • Sunset Boulevard stroll – The beautifully themed Hollywood-era streetscape decorated with winter lights offers a charming, photo-worthy backdrop for couples.

Dining tips

For a romantic sit-down meal, couples should make advance reservations at Hollywood Brown Derby, a sophisticated table-service restaurant inspired by the legendary Tinseltown original, offering refined dishes and an intimate atmosphere. The Hollywood Brown Derby Lounge is a fantastic option for couples wanting to share elegant small plates and signature cocktails without a full dining commitment. Oga's Cantina in Galaxy's Edge provides a lively and unique drinking experience where couples can sip on creative themed cocktails in an immersive Star Wars setting. For a quick but indulgent treat, sharing a Ronto Wrap or browsing the sweet offerings at Trolley Car Café makes for a delightful and casual couples moment between attractions.

Winter planning tips

  • Book early for the holidays – If visiting in December, purchase park tickets and dining reservations well in advance, as the park fills quickly during the festive season.
  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park before opening allows couples to experience high-demand rides like Rise of the Resistance before crowds build through the day.
  • Layer your clothing – Winter mornings and evenings can be chilly, so bring a light jacket or layers to stay comfortable throughout the full day and into the nighttime shows.
  • Use Disney's Lightning Lane – Purchasing Lightning Lane passes for the most popular attractions helps couples maximize their time together rather than spending it waiting in queues.
  • Check the holiday entertainment schedule – Special winter programming including seasonal shows and character experiences are available in December, so review the park calendar ahead of your visit.

How to plan your day

Couples should begin their Hollywood Studios day at rope drop by heading straight to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge to secure spots on Rise of the Resistance and Smugglers Run before mid-morning crowds arrive, then work their way through Toy Story Land and Sunset Boulevard during the afternoon. Use the quieter midday hours to enjoy a leisurely lunch at Hollywood Brown Derby or explore the immersive theming of Galaxy's Edge at a relaxed pace. Cap the evening with Fantasmic!, allowing couples to find a great spot early and end the day with one of Disney's most spectacular and romantically atmospheric nighttime shows.
