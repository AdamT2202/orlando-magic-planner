Universal Studios Florida for Families with young kids in Winter

Everything you need to plan the perfect Universal Studios Florida trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Universal Studios Florida with young kids in winter (December through February) offers a magical experience with cooler, more comfortable temperatures typically ranging from the mid-50s to low 70s Fahrenheit, making it far more manageable for little ones than the sweltering summer heat. Crowd levels vary significantly, with the holiday period around Christmas and New Year bringing some of the busiest days of the year, while January and February tend to be noticeably quieter and easier to navigate with strollers and small children. The park embraces a festive atmosphere in December with Universal's Holiday Parade and seasonal decorations that delight young visitors.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • The Simpsons Ride: A fun, animated simulator adventure that older toddlers and young children enjoy for its colorful characters and gentle-enough motion when seated carefully with a parent.
  • Despicable Me Minion Mayhem: A crowd-pleasing motion simulator featuring beloved Minion characters that young kids absolutely adore, with pre-show entertainment that keeps little ones engaged while waiting.
  • Fievel's Playland: A fantastic water-play-free dry playground area themed around the classic animated mouse, perfect for toddlers and young children to explore at their own pace.
  • Universal's Holiday Parade featuring Macy's (December): A spectacular seasonal parade filled with giant balloons, beloved characters, and festive floats that creates unforgettable memories for young children visiting in December.
  • Animal Actors on Location: A live stage show featuring trained animals performing impressive tricks that keeps young children thoroughly entertained and fully engaged from start to finish.

Dining tips

Universal Studios Florida offers a wide variety of family-friendly dining options that cater well to young children, including kid's menus with familiar favorites like macaroni and cheese, chicken tenders, and mini burgers at restaurants such as Louie's Italian Restaurant and Classic Monster Café. Booking a table-service meal in advance is highly recommended during the busy December holiday period, as popular dining spots fill up quickly and hungry, tired toddlers do not do well with long unexpected waits. Consider purchasing the Universal Dining Plan before your visit to simplify meal payments and help manage your overall budget without the stress of calculating costs mid-day. Packing small snacks like fruit pouches and crackers in your bag is a practical strategy for keeping young kids fueled between meals without relying entirely on park food.

Winter planning tips

  • Layer clothing wisely: Winter mornings in Orlando can feel genuinely chilly, especially for young children, so dress kids in easily removable layers that can be adjusted as temperatures warm through the afternoon.
  • Arrive at rope drop: Getting to the park gates before opening is especially valuable in winter when holiday crowds spike, allowing families to complete the most popular young-child-friendly attractions before lines build significantly.
  • Book a stroller rental or bring your own: Even children who typically walk confidently will tire quickly in a large theme park, and having a stroller available throughout the day prevents meltdowns and keeps the day running smoothly.
  • Download the Universal Orlando app: The app provides real-time wait times, show schedules, and interactive maps that help parents with young kids plan efficient routes and avoid unnecessary backtracking across the park.
  • Plan around nap schedules: If your young child still naps, consider timing a mid-afternoon break at your hotel to recharge before returning refreshed for evening entertainment, especially during December when the park stays open later for holiday events.

How to plan your day

Start your morning at Universal Studios Florida as early as possible by arriving at least 20 minutes before the official opening time, heading directly to Despicable Me Minion Mayhem and the Simpsons Ride before crowds from late-arriving families build up in the mid-morning hours. Use the quieter early afternoon to enjoy character meet-and-greets, live shows like Animal Actors on Location, and a relaxed sit-down lunch where young kids can rest their legs and decompress in a calmer environment. If visiting in December, plan your evening around the Holiday Parade and festive character experiences, keeping in mind that young children tend to have the most energy and best temperament earlier in the day.
