Hollywood Studios for Adults-only groups in Winter

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios during the winter months of December through February offers a thrilling and immersive experience for adults-only groups, with the park buzzing with festive energy during the holiday season before settling into a more relaxed pace in January and February. Crowd levels peak sharply around Christmas and New Year but noticeably drop after mid-January, making late winter an ideal sweet spot for groups who want shorter wait times and a more laid-back atmosphere. Temperatures are mild and comfortable, typically ranging from the low 60s to mid-70s Fahrenheit, making it easy to explore the park all day without the intense heat of summer.

Top attractions for Adults-only groups

  • Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance – A jaw-dropping, fully immersive ride that delivers a cinematic scale and intensity that adult fans of the franchise will absolutely love.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – An interactive cockpit experience that rewards teamwork and friendly competition, perfect for a group dynamic.
  • Tower of Terror – A classic thrill ride with a sophisticated horror-hotel storyline that resonates far more with adults than younger visitors.
  • Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring Aerosmith – A high-speed indoor coaster with an electric soundtrack that becomes an instant favourite for groups seeking adrenaline.
  • Savi's Workshop – Handcrafted Lightsabers – A premium, reservation-based experience that offers adults a deeply personal and memorable Star Wars keepsake worth the splurge.

Dining tips

Adults-only groups visiting Hollywood Studios in winter should prioritise an advance dining reservation at Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant, where retro drive-in booths and classic B-movie clips create an atmospheric and uniquely adult dining experience. For a more upscale option, The Hollywood Brown Derby delivers sophisticated American cuisine and craft cocktails in an elegant setting inspired by the original Tinseltown landmark. Groups looking to keep things casual should explore the Galaxy's Edge area, where unique themed beverages and quick-service bites at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo provide a fun, leisurely meal with plenty of character. Don't overlook Oga's Cantina for pre-dinner drinks, as this ticketed Star Wars bar offers creative alcoholic cocktails and a lively atmosphere that sets the tone for a memorable evening.

Winter planning tips

  • Arrive early during the holiday period – The weeks surrounding Christmas and New Year see some of the highest attendance of the year, so plan to be at the gates before opening to secure boarding groups and Lightning Lane passes for top attractions.
  • Book Oga's Cantina in advance – Reservations for this popular Star Wars bar fill up weeks ahead, especially during peak winter dates, so secure your spot through the Disney app as early as possible.
  • Layer your clothing – Florida evenings in January and February can dip into the low 50s, so bring a light jacket or hoodie to stay comfortable during evening shows and fireworks.
  • Take advantage of late January and February crowds – If your schedule allows flexibility, visiting after the Martin Luther King Jr. holiday weekend delivers noticeably thinner crowds and reduced wait times across the whole park.
  • Check for Disney After Hours events – Winter often sees special ticketed evening events at Hollywood Studios that give adults near-exclusive access to major attractions with minimal queues.

How to plan your day

Adults-only groups should begin their day by heading straight to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge to tackle Rise of the Resistance and Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run before crowds build, then work through Toy Story Land and the major thrill rides during the mid-morning lull. Use the early afternoon for a leisurely lunch, a visit to Savi's Workshop if booked, and a browse through the immersive themed retail areas, allowing the biggest queues of the day to pass before returning to any remaining must-do attractions. As evening approaches, position your group for the spectacular nighttime shows including Fantasmic! and the Star Wars: A Galactic Spectacular fireworks display, which provide a dramatic and fittingly cinematic close to a well-structured day.
