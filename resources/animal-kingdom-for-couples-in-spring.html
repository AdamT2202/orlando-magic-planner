Spring · March–May

Animal Kingdom for Couples in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Animal Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Spring is one of the most magical times for couples to visit Disney's Animal Kingdom, with lush greenery, blooming landscapes, and warm temperatures creating a naturally romantic backdrop. Crowd levels tend to build through spring break in late March and early April before easing slightly in May, so visiting on weekdays or arriving early can make a significant difference. The pleasant daytime temperatures ranging from the mid-70s to mid-80s°F make exploring the park's expansive outdoor trails and immersive lands genuinely enjoyable for two.

Top attractions for Couples

  • Avatar Flight of Passage: This breathtaking ride through Pandora offers a jaw-dropping shared experience that feels tailor-made for couples looking for an unforgettable thrill together.
  • Kilimanjaro Safaris: A serene and romantic way to witness majestic wildlife up close, with the golden spring light making for stunning shared memories and photos.
  • Na'vi River Journey: A gentle, visually stunning boat ride through the bioluminescent world of Pandora that offers couples a peaceful and immersive escape.
  • Expedition Everest: A classic high-energy roller coaster that delivers shared screams and laughs, perfect for adventurous couples bonding over thrills.
  • The Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail: A slow, intimate walking trail where couples can observe gorillas, exotic birds, and hippos at their own relaxed pace away from the busier crowds.

Dining tips

For a truly special dining experience, couples should book a table at Tiffins Restaurant, Animal Kingdom's signature fine-dining spot offering globally inspired cuisine in an elegant, art-filled setting that feels worlds apart from typical theme park dining. Nomad Lounge, located adjacent to Tiffins, is a hidden gem for couples seeking handcrafted cocktails and shareable small plates in a relaxed, breezy atmosphere overlooking the Discovery River. Satu'li Canteen in Pandora provides a more casual but visually immersive meal option with healthy, flavorful bowls that are perfect for refuelling mid-adventure. Advance dining reservations are strongly recommended for spring visits, especially for Tiffins, as tables fill up quickly during the busier spring travel season.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop: Getting to the park at opening allows couples to experience top attractions like Flight of Passage with minimal wait times before spring break crowds build throughout the day.
  • Book Lightning Lane early: Secure Lightning Lane passes for the most in-demand rides as soon as the park opens to avoid spending your romantic day standing in long queues.
  • Pack for afternoon rain: Florida spring afternoons frequently bring brief showers, so a compact umbrella or light rain ponchos can keep couples comfortable without derailing plans.
  • Visit Pandora at dusk: The bioluminescent lighting effects in Pandora become fully visible as evening falls, creating one of the most stunning and romantic visual experiences in any theme park.
  • Check the Entertainment Schedule: Spring brings live performances and special character experiences to Animal Kingdom, so reviewing the daily schedule in advance helps couples catch unique moments worth planning around.

How to plan your day

Couples should begin their day in Pandora at rope drop, tackling Avatar Flight of Passage and Na'vi River Journey back-to-back before heading to Africa for Kilimanjaro Safaris while the morning light is still soft and the animals are most active. Spend the midday hours exploring walking trails, enjoying a leisurely lunch at Tiffins or Nomad Lounge, and ducking into air-conditioned shows to escape the peak afternoon heat common in spring. End the evening back in Pandora as darkness falls to witness the glowing bioluminescent landscape together, providing a breathtaking and genuinely romantic conclusion to your Animal Kingdom day.
