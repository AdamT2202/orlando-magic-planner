Hollywood Studios for Families with teens in Winter

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in winter draws moderate to heavy crowds, particularly around the holiday season in December when festive decorations and special events like Jingle Bell, Jingle BAM! create a magical atmosphere for families with teens. January and February offer a welcome relief with noticeably thinner crowds, making it easier to tackle major attractions without excessive wait times. Temperatures typically range from the mid-50s to low 70s Fahrenheit, making for comfortable park days that teens and adults alike will appreciate compared to the sweltering summer heat.

Top attractions for Families with teens

  • Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance – An immersive, large-scale experience that drops riders into the heart of a Star Wars battle, thrilling teens with its cutting-edge storytelling and stunning effects.
  • Mickey and Minnie's Runaway Railway – A fun, trackless ride with unpredictable twists that keeps teens engaged while offering a visually inventive experience the whole family can enjoy together.
  • Tower of Terror – A classic Hollywood Studios thrill that delivers serious drop sequences and eerie atmosphere, making it a must for adventure-seeking teens.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – Teens love the interactive co-pilot experience where their choices actually affect the outcome, adding a competitive edge to the ride.
  • Slinky Dog Dash – A surprisingly exciting roller coaster set in Toy Story Land that bridges the gap between younger siblings and thrill-hungry teens perfectly.

Dining tips

Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant is a teen favourite, offering classic American comfort food in a retro drive-in setting complete with movie clips on a big screen, making mealtime genuinely entertaining. The Backlot Express is a great quick-service option for families who want to keep moving, with burgers, chicken strips, and loaded fries that satisfy hungry teens without a long wait. For a more indulgent treat, Woody's Lunch Box in Toy Story Land serves nostalgic favourites like totchos and lunch box tarts that teens tend to love. Booking table-service restaurants in advance is strongly recommended during December when holiday crowds push dining wait times significantly higher.

Winter planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park before opening allows families to tackle high-demand rides like Rise of the Resistance before midday crowds build up significantly.
  • Layer your clothing – December and January mornings can be genuinely chilly, so bring light jackets or hoodies that can be stowed in a bag as temperatures rise through the afternoon.
  • Purchase Lightning Lane passes strategically – Use Individual Lightning Lane for the highest-demand Star Wars attractions and reserve them as early as possible, especially during the busy holiday weeks.
  • Check the holiday entertainment schedule – December brings special nighttime shows and seasonal overlays that are worth planning your evening around for a memorable family experience.
  • Visit in January or February for the best value – Post-holiday crowds drop considerably, park hours may be shorter but waits are dramatically reduced, and hotel rates around Orlando tend to be far more affordable.

How to plan your day

Start your morning at Galaxy's Edge to tackle Rise of the Resistance and Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run at rope drop while waits are shortest, then work your way through Toy Story Land before crowds migrate in that direction around mid-morning. Plan a sit-down lunch at a table-service restaurant as a midday break, which also conveniently sidesteps the busiest ride queue periods between noon and 3 PM. Use the late afternoon and evening to revisit favourites with shorter waits and to catch any nighttime spectaculars or seasonal shows that make a Hollywood Studios winter visit truly special for teens and families.
