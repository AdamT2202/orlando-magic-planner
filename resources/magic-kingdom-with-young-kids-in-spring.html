Spring · March–May

Magic Kingdom for Families with young kids in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Magic Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Spring is one of the most magical times to visit Magic Kingdom with young children, as the park bursts with vibrant seasonal decorations and a lively, festive atmosphere that little ones absolutely love. Crowd levels tend to be moderate to high, particularly during spring break weeks in March and April, so arriving early and planning ahead is essential for families. The weather in Orlando during March through May is generally warm and pleasant, with temperatures ranging from the mid-60s to the mid-80s°F, making it comfortable for strollers and little legs exploring the park all day.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Dumbo the Flying Elephant – A gentle, spinning ride that young children absolutely adore, offering a slow and scenic aerial view of Fantasyland with zero scare factor.
  • The Many Adventures of Winnie the Pooh – A sweet and colourful dark ride through the Hundred Acre Wood that delights toddlers and preschoolers with beloved characters and cheerful storytelling.
  • Peter Pan's Flight – A timeless classic that whisks little ones over glittering London and Neverland in a flying pirate ship, sparking genuine wonder and excitement.
  • Mickey's PhilharMagic – A fully immersive 4D show featuring favourite Disney characters and gentle sensory effects, perfect for young kids who may not be ready for bigger rides.
  • Seven Dwarfs Mine Train – A mild, family-friendly roller coaster with a 38-inch height requirement that serves as a wonderful first coaster experience for slightly older young children.

Dining tips

Magic Kingdom offers several family-friendly dining options that cater perfectly to picky young eaters, including familiar favourites like pizza, macaroni and cheese, and grilled chicken at quick-service locations such as Pinocchio Village Haus and Cosmic Ray's Starlight Café. For a truly special and memorable experience, booking a character dining reservation at The Crystal Palace allows young children to meet Winnie the Pooh and friends over a relaxed buffet meal without the stress of long meet-and-greet queues. Be sure to make dining reservations well in advance, especially for spring break periods, as popular restaurants fill up weeks ahead of time. Packing small snacks in your bag can also help keep energy levels up between meals and prevent meltdowns during busy spring days.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive before park opening – Getting through the gates early during spring means shorter queues for the most popular young-child attractions before crowds build throughout the day.
  • Use the Disney Genie+ service – Lightning Lane selections can save significant time for families with young kids, reducing frustrating waits and keeping little ones happy and engaged.
  • Plan around nap and rest times – Schedule a mid-afternoon break at your resort hotel to avoid the hottest part of the day and recharge young children for a refreshed evening return to the park.
  • Dress in breathable layers – Spring mornings can be cool while afternoons heat up quickly, so lightweight layers and comfortable shoes are essential for the whole family.
  • Download the My Disney Experience app – Real-time wait times, interactive maps, and dining reservations are all accessible in one place, making navigation with young children far less stressful.

How to plan your day

Start your morning by heading straight to Fantasyland when the park opens, tackling high-demand rides like Peter Pan's Flight and Seven Dwarfs Mine Train before queues grow during the busy spring season. Schedule a character dining lunch or a sit-down midday break to rest young legs, then use the cooler evening hours to enjoy the iconic parades, the spectacular Happily Ever After fireworks show, and gentler attractions with shorter end-of-day queues. Keeping your itinerary flexible and focused on your children's energy levels rather than trying to see everything will ensure a genuinely joyful and stress-free Magic Kingdom experience for the whole family.
