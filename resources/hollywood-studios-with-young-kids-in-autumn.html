Hollywood Studios for Families with young kids in Autumn

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Hollywood Studios with young kids during autumn is a fantastic experience, as September through November brings noticeably lower crowd levels compared to the peak summer months, making it easier to enjoy attractions without lengthy waits. The Florida weather begins to cool slightly by October and November, with temperatures ranging from the mid-70s to low 80s Fahrenheit, offering a much more comfortable experience for little ones than the intense summer heat. The park also comes alive with festive seasonal atmosphere, including Halloween and holiday decorations that young children absolutely love.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Toy Story Land – This entire land is perfectly tailored for young children, with rides like Slinky Dog Dash and Alien Swirling Saucers offering gentle thrills that toddlers and preschoolers can enjoy together with their parents.
  • Muppet*Vision 3D – A fun, immersive 3D show that entertains young kids with beloved Muppet characters, colourful visuals, and silly humour without any scary elements.
  • Disney Junior Dance Party – Young children will be thrilled to dance and sing alongside characters from their favourite Disney Junior shows in this energetic, interactive live experience.
  • Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge – While some rides have height restrictions, young kids adore the spectacular, immersive environment, especially meeting characters and exploring the unique otherworldly setting.
  • Voyage of the Little Mermaid – A gentle, darkened puppet and projection show featuring beloved characters from The Little Mermaid that captivates younger children with its magical undersea storytelling.

Dining tips

Hollywood Studios offers several family-friendly dining options perfectly suited to picky young eaters, with Woody's Lunch Box in Toy Story Land being a top pick for kids thanks to its fun theming, manageable portions, and crowd-pleasing menu items like grilled cheese sandwiches and totchos. The Backlot Express near Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge is a quick-service favourite offering classic American comfort foods including burgers and chicken tenders that young children reliably enjoy. For a more relaxed sit-down meal, the Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant delivers a uniquely entertaining experience for families, with cars to sit in while watching classic movie clips, keeping young kids engaged throughout the meal. Be sure to make advance dining reservations for table-service restaurants as soon as your booking window opens, even during the quieter autumn season, as popular spots fill up quickly.

Autumn planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park right at opening during autumn means you can tackle Slinky Dog Dash and other popular rides before crowds build up, even on busier days around school holiday breaks in October.
  • Book Lightning Lane selections early – Use the Disney Genie+ service and book Lightning Lane passes for the most in-demand family attractions first thing in the morning to avoid disappointment later in the day.
  • Pack layers for cooler evenings – While autumn days remain warm, evenings in October and November can feel surprisingly cool for young children, so bring lightweight jackets or hoodies for after dark.
  • Plan around nap times – Use the midday lull, which is typically the warmest and busiest part of the day, as a natural break back at your hotel before returning refreshed for the cooler, less crowded evening hours.
  • Check the park calendar for special events – Autumn hosts seasonal events that can affect park hours and crowd patterns, so review the official Disney calendar before your visit to plan accordingly.

How to plan your day

Start your morning at rope drop by heading straight to Toy Story Land to ride Slinky Dog Dash and Alien Swirling Saucers before lines grow, then move on to the Disney Junior Dance Party and Muppet*Vision 3D during the midmorning when young children are at their most energetic and engaged. Take a midday break during the warmest hours to rest, recharge, and enjoy a relaxed lunch before returning to the park in the late afternoon to explore Galaxy's Edge and catch any character meet-and-greets. Wrap up your evening by revisiting favourite rides with shorter autumn queues and staying for the nighttime shows, which provide a truly magical finale that young kids will talk about long after the trip is over.
