Hollywood Studios for Families with teens in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in spring is an exciting destination for families with teens, offering a high-energy atmosphere packed with immersive lands and thrilling attractions. Crowd levels tend to rise during spring break weeks in March and April, so expect busier park days, particularly around Easter weekend. Weather is warm and pleasant, typically ranging from the mid-70s to mid-80s°F, with occasional afternoon showers that are easy to plan around.

Top attractions for Families with teens

  • Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance – This jaw-dropping, multi-sensory ride is a must for teens who love epic storytelling and cutting-edge theme park technology.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – Teens love the interactive cockpit experience where their choices actually affect the outcome of the mission.
  • Tower of Terror – A classic thrill ride that drops riders in a haunted elevator shaft, delivering screams and laughs in equal measure.
  • Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring Aerosmith – A high-speed indoor coaster with inversions and a rock soundtrack that appeals to older kids and teens.
  • Slinky Dog Dash – A fun family coaster in Toy Story Land that bridges the gap between younger siblings and thrill-seeking teens perfectly.

Dining tips

Hollywood Studios has a strong dining scene that caters well to hungry teens with big appetites and adventurous tastes. Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant is a teen favorite, featuring vintage drive-in movie clips and classic American comfort food served in retro cars. For a quick-service option, Woody's Lunch Box in Toy Story Land offers hearty toasted sandwiches and nostalgic lunch box tarts that teens tend to love. Make advance dining reservations as early as possible for table-service restaurants, especially during the busy spring break period when spots fill up fast.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive before park opening – Early entry gives families a head start on boarding top attractions like Rise of the Resistance before lines grow throughout the day.
  • Use Lightning Lane strategically – Purchase Lightning Lane passes for the most in-demand rides to avoid long standby waits during peak spring crowds.
  • Plan around afternoon showers – Spring brings brief but heavy rainstorms, so schedule indoor attractions or dining during the typical mid-afternoon weather window.
  • Book dining reservations 60 days out – Table-service restaurants book up quickly during spring break season, so plan ahead to secure your preferred dining times.
  • Download the My Disney Experience app – Real-time wait times and mobile ordering through the app help families maximize their day with minimal stress.

How to plan your day

Start your morning by heading straight to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge to experience Rise of the Resistance and Millennium Falcon before wait times peak, then move through Sunset Boulevard to tackle Tower of Terror and Rock 'n' Roller Coaster mid-morning. Use the early afternoon for dining, exploring Star Wars merch, and catching live entertainment such as the Indiana Jones Epic Stunt Spectacular, which offers a welcome seated break during the hottest part of the day. Return to any must-do attractions in the evening when crowds typically thin out and the park takes on a magical atmosphere under the lights.
