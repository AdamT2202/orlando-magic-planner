Hollywood Studios for Families with teens in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in summer is a buzzing, high-energy experience that teens tend to absolutely love, with blockbuster attractions and immersive lands creating an atmosphere that feels more thrilling theme park than family-friendly resort. Crowds peak significantly between June and August as school holidays bring families from across the country and beyond, so expect longer wait times at the most popular rides. The Florida heat and humidity are intense during these months, with temperatures frequently climbing above 90°F, but frequent afternoon thunderstorms can provide brief relief and thin out the queues.

Top attractions for Families with teens

  • Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance – An epic, fully immersive ride that drops guests into a battle between the Resistance and the First Order, delivering a cinematic experience teens will talk about for years.
  • Tower of Terror – This classic drop tower delivers unpredictable free-falls and a haunting storyline that thrill-seeking teens consistently rate as a park highlight.
  • Slinky Dog Dash – A surprisingly fun family coaster with enough speed and airtime to satisfy teens while remaining accessible for the whole group.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – Teens love the interactive cockpit experience where their choices genuinely affect the outcome of the mission, adding real replay value.
  • Rock 'n' Roller Coaster starring Aerosmith – A launched indoor coaster with multiple inversions and a hard-rock soundtrack that makes it a must-ride for thrill-seeking teens.

Dining tips

Hollywood Studios offers some of Walt Disney World's most themed and satisfying dining, making mealtimes part of the experience rather than a break from it. Teens tend to gravitate toward Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo in Galaxy's Edge, where the creative alien-themed dishes feel genuinely cool and Instagram-worthy. ABC Commissary and Backlot Express offer quick-service options with broader menus that keep picky eaters happy without a long wait. Booking a table-service reservation at Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater well in advance is highly recommended, as the drive-in movie setting paired with classic sci-fi B-movie clips creates a memorable dining experience teens genuinely enjoy.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park gates 30 minutes before official opening allows your family to ride the most popular attractions before queues build to peak lengths.
  • Use Lightning Lane strategically – Purchase Lightning Lane passes for Rise of the Resistance and Tower of Terror early in the day, as availability for these top-tier rides sells out fast in summer.
  • Plan a midday break – The hottest and most crowded period runs from 11am to 4pm, so consider returning to your resort for lunch and a swim before heading back for evening entertainment.
  • Dress for the heat – Light, moisture-wicking clothing, sunscreen, and refillable water bottles are essential; portable cooling fans and cooling towels can make a significant difference during peak afternoon heat.
  • Check the park calendar – Summer evenings sometimes feature special events or extended hours for Disney resort guests, giving your family extra ride time when day visitors have left.

How to plan your day

Start your morning with a focused sprint to either Rise of the Resistance or Rock 'n' Roller Coaster at rope drop, then work through the remaining high-priority attractions before midday crowds make waits prohibitive. Use the early afternoon for dining, shopping in Galaxy's Edge, and any shows or experiences with shorter queues, treating the hottest hours as a slower, more relaxed exploration of the park's incredible theming. Return with renewed energy in the early evening when the heat eases, crowds thin slightly, and the park's dramatic lighting transforms Hollywood Boulevard into a spectacular backdrop for your family's final rides of the day.
