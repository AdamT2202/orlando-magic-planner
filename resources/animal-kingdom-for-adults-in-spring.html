Animal Kingdom for Adults-only groups in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Animal Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Animal Kingdom with an adults-only group in spring means enjoying the park during one of Orlando's most vibrant seasons, with lush greenery, blooming landscapes, and warm but manageable temperatures typically ranging from the mid-60s to the mid-80s Fahrenheit. Crowd levels tend to build through spring break periods in March and early April, then ease slightly in late April and May, giving savvy groups pockets of quieter park time to take full advantage of. The atmosphere is energetic and immersive, with the park's wildlife, theatrical entertainment, and adventure-focused experiences feeling especially rewarding when you can explore at an adult pace without the constraints of young children.

Top attractions for Adults-only groups

  • Avatar Flight of Passage – An extraordinary and intense 3D flying simulator that consistently ranks as one of the best theme park attractions in the world, offering a breathtaking experience best appreciated by thrill-seeking adults.
  • Expedition Everest – A fast-paced roller coaster through a convincingly detailed Himalayan mountain setting, combining genuine speed, backwards drops, and impressive theming that adults find endlessly rerideable.
  • Kilimanjaro Safaris – A sprawling open-vehicle safari through authentic African savanna landscapes where real animals roam freely, offering a relaxed yet genuinely awe-inspiring experience that resonates deeply with adult visitors.
  • Rivers of Light: We Are One – A spectacular nighttime show combining live music, floating lanterns, water screens, and animal imagery that creates a sophisticated and emotionally moving end to the evening for adult groups.
  • Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail – A self-guided walking trail through lush habitats housing gorillas, hippos, and exotic birds that rewards adults who prefer a slower, more contemplative wildlife-watching experience.

Dining tips

Adults-only groups should prioritize a reservation at Tiffins Restaurant, Animal Kingdom's signature fine dining establishment, which offers globally inspired cuisine, an impressive cocktail and wine list, and a refined atmosphere that stands out among all Walt Disney World dining options. Nomad Lounge, located adjacent to Tiffins, is an excellent spot for craft cocktails, shareable small plates, and a relaxed riverside setting that works perfectly for an afternoon break or a pre-dinner drink with the group. For a more casual but equally enjoyable meal, Satu'li Canteen in Pandora offers customizable bowls with bold flavors and a visually stunning environment that adults consistently rave about. Making advance dining reservations at least 60 days out is strongly recommended during spring, particularly for Tiffins, as tables fill quickly during the busy season.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park before opening allows adults-only groups to experience Flight of Passage and Expedition Everest with significantly shorter wait times before the midday crowds arrive.
  • Use Lightning Lane strategically – Purchase individual Lightning Lane access for Avatar Flight of Passage early in the morning, as this attraction consistently sells out and wait times regularly exceed 90 minutes by mid-morning.
  • Plan around spring break peaks – The last two weeks of March and the first two weeks of April see the heaviest crowds, so aim for late April or early May for a noticeably more comfortable visit.
  • Dress in moisture-wicking layers – Spring mornings can be cool while afternoons heat up quickly, and brief afternoon rain showers are common in May, so lightweight layers and a compact rain poncho are essential packing items.
  • Stay for the evening – Animal Kingdom's nighttime offerings, including illuminated Pandora and evening entertainment, are best experienced by adults who can linger past the dinner hour without worrying about children's bedtimes.

How to plan your day

Adults-only groups should begin the day in Pandora – The World of Avatar immediately at park opening, securing rides on Flight of Passage and Na'vi River Journey before the queues swell, then move through Africa and Asia to tackle Kilimanjaro Safaris and Expedition Everest during the mid-morning lull. Use the warmer midday hours for a leisurely lunch at Tiffins or drinks at Nomad Lounge, followed by the Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail and any shows or live entertainment that appeal to the group. As the afternoon cools, revisit favourite attractions with reduced wait times, position yourselves in Pandora for the stunning dusk lighting transformation, and close the evening with Rivers of Light for a genuinely memorable adult Disney experience.
