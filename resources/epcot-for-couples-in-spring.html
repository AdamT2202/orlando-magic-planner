Spring · March–May

EPCOT for Couples in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect EPCOT trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting EPCOT as a couple in spring offers a magical blend of blooming festivals, comfortable temperatures, and a romantic atmosphere that makes it one of the best times of year to explore the park together. March through May brings the internationally celebrated EPCOT International Flower and Garden Festival, filling the park with stunning topiaries, outdoor kitchens, and live entertainment that creates a naturally intimate and visually stunning backdrop. Crowd levels tend to be moderate, with spring break periods in late March and early April seeing higher attendance, while weekdays in May offer a noticeably quieter and more relaxed experience.

Top attractions for Couples

  • Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind – A thrilling reverse-launch roller coaster perfect for couples who love sharing an adrenaline rush set to an epic soundtrack.
  • Remy's Ratatouille Adventure – A charming and whimsical ride through a Parisian kitchen that sparks joy and nostalgia, ideal for couples who enjoy lighthearted fun.
  • Frozen Ever After – A beautiful boat ride through the kingdom of Arendelle that offers a peaceful and enchanting shared experience.
  • World Showcase Promenade – Strolling hand-in-hand through eleven international pavilions allows couples to discover unique foods, drinks, and cultures together at their own pace.
  • EPCOT Forever nighttime spectacular – Watching the stunning fireworks and illuminations show over World Showcase Lagoon is an undeniably romantic way to end the evening together.

Dining tips

EPCOT is widely regarded as one of the finest dining destinations in all of Walt Disney World, making it a dream for couples who appreciate good food and drink. Reserve a table well in advance at romantic restaurants such as Monsieur Paul in the France Pavilion, which offers refined French cuisine and an elegant atmosphere, or Tutto Italia Ristorante for candlelit Italian dining in the Italy Pavilion. The Flower and Garden Festival's outdoor kitchens are a wonderful option for couples who enjoy grazing through small plates and craft beverages while wandering the park together. For a truly special occasion, consider the EPCOT experience dining package, which includes a reserved viewing area for the nighttime spectacular.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive early on weekdays – Weekday mornings in May are significantly less crowded, giving couples the chance to experience popular attractions like Guardians of the Galaxy with minimal wait times.
  • Book dining reservations 60 days ahead – The most sought-after EPCOT restaurants fill up quickly, so planning well in advance ensures couples secure the best tables for a memorable evening.
  • Dress in light, breathable layers – Spring temperatures in Orlando typically range from the mid-60s to the upper 80s Fahrenheit, so lightweight clothing with a light jacket for air-conditioned attractions is ideal.
  • Check the Flower and Garden Festival schedule – Live musical performances at the Garden Rocks Concert Series are included with park admission and offer a wonderful free entertainment option for couples in the evenings.
  • Avoid spring break peak weeks – The last week of March and first two weeks of April see the highest attendance of the season, so if flexibility allows, visiting before or after these dates will significantly improve the experience.

How to plan your day

Begin your day at EPCOT by heading straight to Future World to ride Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind and Remy's Ratatouille Adventure during the first hour when lines are shortest, then transition into a leisurely mid-morning exploration of the Flower and Garden Festival topiaries and outdoor kitchens. Spend the afternoon meandering through the World Showcase pavilions at a relaxed pace, sampling food and drinks from different countries and soaking in the unique cultural entertainment each nation offers. As evening approaches, find a prime spot along the World Showcase Lagoon to enjoy a cocktail and watch the park transform under the glow of the nighttime spectacular, creating an effortlessly romantic conclusion to a perfectly curated day together.
