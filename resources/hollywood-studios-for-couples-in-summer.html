Hollywood Studios for Couples in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in summer draws large crowds, particularly from families on school vacation, but couples can still enjoy a magical and immersive experience with the right strategy. June through August brings intense Florida heat and humidity, with afternoon thunderstorms that are brief but frequent, so planning around the weather is essential. The atmosphere buzzes with energy, live entertainment, and the electric excitement of Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge and other signature lands that make the park a thrilling destination for couples seeking adventure together.

Top attractions for Couples

  • Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance – An unmissable, cinematic ride experience that feels like stepping into a movie together, perfect for couples who love epic shared adventures.
  • Tower of Terror – A classic thrill ride that delivers screams and laughter in equal measure, making it an ideal bonding experience for couples who enjoy adrenaline.
  • Fantasmic! Nighttime Spectacular – A romantic and dramatic outdoor show that combines water, fire, and beloved Disney characters, best enjoyed together in the evening.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – An interactive cockpit experience where couples can work as a team, adding a fun and playful competitive element to the day.
  • Indiana Jones Epic Stunt Spectacular – A thrilling live-action show that couples can enjoy side by side with impressive stunts and storytelling in an open-air theater.

Dining tips

Oga's Cantina in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge is one of the most unique and intimate dining spots in all of Walt Disney World, offering themed cocktails and small bites in an atmospheric bar setting that couples absolutely love. Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant provides a wonderfully nostalgic and romantic experience, seating guests in vintage car booths while black-and-white sci-fi films play on a giant screen under a simulated starlit sky. For a more upscale and relaxed meal, The Hollywood Brown Derby serves classic American cuisine in an elegant, Hollywood golden-age inspired setting that suits a special date-night atmosphere. Booking advance dining reservations through the My Disney Experience app is strongly recommended during summer, as popular restaurants fill up weeks ahead.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive at park opening to ride the most popular attractions like Rise of the Resistance before crowds and heat build throughout the morning.
  • Use the Lightning Lane Multi Pass and Individual Lightning Lane options to minimize wait times for top rides during the busiest summer peak periods.
  • Plan for a mid-afternoon break, either returning to your resort or ducking into air-conditioned shows and restaurants during the hottest hours between noon and 3pm.
  • Pack a light poncho or small umbrella to handle the short but heavy afternoon thunderstorms that are common throughout June, July, and August in Orlando.
  • Stay for the evening, as summer nights at Hollywood Studios offer cooler temperatures, spectacular lighting on the park's facades, and the unmissable Fantasmic! show.

How to plan your day

Couples should begin their Hollywood Studios day early by heading straight to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge to experience Rise of the Resistance and Millennium Falcon before the midday rush, then work through other high-priority attractions before retreating to a sit-down lunch with air conditioning during the peak afternoon heat. Use the slower mid-afternoon hours to explore the Commissary Lane area, enjoy a leisurely drink at Oga's Cantina, or catch live entertainment and shows that offer a welcome cool-down break. End the evening with a table-service dinner followed by Fantasmic!, creating a natural and memorable romantic conclusion to a full and exciting day at the park.
