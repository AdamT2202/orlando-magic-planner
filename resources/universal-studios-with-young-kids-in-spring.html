Universal Studios Florida for Families with young kids in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Universal Studios Florida trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Universal Studios Florida with young kids in spring means warm, sunny weather with temperatures typically ranging from the mid-60s to the mid-80s°F, creating a comfortable and exciting atmosphere for little explorers. Crowds begin to build in March around spring break season, so expect busier park days in late March and mid-April, while early March and early May tend to offer a more relaxed experience. The park comes alive with vibrant energy during spring, making it a magical time for families to enjoy themed lands, live entertainment, and character meet-and-greets.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • DreamWorks Destination: Young children love meeting beloved characters from Shrek, Trolls, and Kung Fu Panda up close, making it a must-visit spot for little fans.
  • The Secret Life of Pets: Off the Leash! This gentle ride is perfectly sized for toddlers and young children, offering fun storytelling in a non-intimidating format.
  • Despicable Me Minion Mayhem: A family-friendly motion simulator that delights young kids with humor, bright visuals, and the beloved Minion characters.
  • Fievel's Playland: An oversized splash-and-play zone inspired by An American Tail that gives young kids a safe, imaginative space to burn off energy.
  • Hogwarts Express (with park-to-park ticket): Even young children are captivated by the immersive storytelling experience of riding between Diagon Alley and Hogsmeade.

Dining tips

Universal Studios Florida offers a wide variety of family-friendly dining options that cater well to picky young eaters, with kid's meals available at most quick-service and sit-down restaurants throughout the park. For a fun and convenient option, Mel's Drive-In in the Hollywood area serves classic burgers, hot dogs, and chicken strips in a retro diner setting that kids absolutely love. If your family wants a more relaxed sit-down experience, Finnegan's Bar and Grill offers a quieter atmosphere where parents can recharge while children enjoy simple, familiar menu items. Consider dining during off-peak hours, such as 11:00 AM or after 2:00 PM, to avoid long waits and keep young kids from getting overtired and hungry in long lunch lines.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive early: Gates open before official park hours for on-site hotel guests, giving families with young kids a crucial head start before spring break crowds arrive.
  • Book tickets in advance: Spring is one of the busiest seasons at Universal Orlando, so purchasing tickets and any Express Passes well ahead of your trip is strongly recommended.
  • Pack for variable weather: Spring afternoons frequently bring brief rain showers, so carry a compact stroller rain cover and ponchos to keep young children dry without leaving the park.
  • Plan for afternoon rest breaks: Young kids can tire quickly in Florida's spring heat, so build in time for a mid-afternoon break back at your hotel or a quieter area of the park.
  • Download the Universal Orlando app: Use the app to check live wait times, locate character meet-and-greet schedules, and find baby care centers conveniently located throughout the park.

How to plan your day

Start your morning as early as possible by heading straight to the most popular family attractions like Despicable Me Minion Mayhem and The Secret Life of Pets before crowds peak, typically around 11:00 AM during spring break periods. Schedule character meet-and-greets and slower-paced activities like Fievel's Playland and live shows for the busy midday hours when ride wait times are at their longest. Wind down your evening with a leisurely stroll through the park's themed areas, enjoy an early dinner, and plan to catch the evening entertainment before heading back to your hotel with happy, well-rested young children.
