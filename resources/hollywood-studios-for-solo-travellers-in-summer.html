Hollywood Studios for Solo travellers in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in summer draws some of the largest crowds of the year, so solo travellers should be prepared for busy queues, especially at flagship attractions like Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge and Toy Story Land. The Florida heat between June and August is intense, with temperatures regularly exceeding 90°F and afternoon thunderstorms arriving almost daily, making strategic planning essential. Despite the heat and crowds, the park buzzes with an electric atmosphere, and solo visitors benefit from the flexibility to move quickly, grab last-minute ride slots, and soak up the immersive theming at their own pace.

Top attractions for Solo travellers

  • Rise of the Resistance – An unmissable, large-scale Star Wars experience that solo travellers can join via the virtual queue with ease, as single riders often secure spots faster than groups.
  • Tower of Terror – A thrilling drop ride where solo visitors frequently fill single seats quickly, often resulting in shorter effective wait times.
  • MicroStroller the Amazing Adventures of Spider-Man – A high-energy, immersive dark ride that is equally enjoyable alone and rewards the focused attention a solo visitor can give it.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – Solo travellers love this interactive cockpit experience, and single riders are often paired with groups, cutting queue time significantly.
  • Indiana Jones Epic Stunt Spectacular – A fantastic live show that solo guests can enjoy without coordinating group seating, arriving just 15 minutes early for a great spot.

Dining tips

Solo travellers at Hollywood Studios will find the park's counter-service options ideal, with Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo in Galaxy's Edge offering creative, themed dishes that are easy to enjoy alone at communal seating areas. Woody's Lunch Box in Toy Story Land is a beloved quick-service spot with affordable, satisfying bites perfect for a solo mid-morning refuel before the afternoon rush hits. For a sit-down experience, the 50's Prime Time Café offers interactive, character-driven dining that is particularly fun for solo guests who enjoy engaging with the theatrical wait staff. Booking a table-service restaurant in advance through the My Disney Experience app is strongly recommended during summer, as popular spots fill up weeks ahead of time.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive before park opening – Rope-dropping Hollywood Studios gives solo travellers a critical head start on Rise of the Resistance and Slinky Dog Dash before heat and crowds peak.
  • Use the Lightning Lane – Purchasing Lightning Lane selections for the top two or three must-do attractions will save significant time during the busy summer period.
  • Plan around afternoon storms – Schedule indoor shows, dining, and merchandise browsing between 2pm and 4pm when Florida thunderstorms are most likely to roll in.
  • Stay hydrated and use cooling stations – Carry a refillable water bottle, take advantage of free cups of ice water at any counter-service location, and seek out air-conditioned spaces regularly.
  • Download the My Disney Experience app – Solo travellers can monitor live wait times, secure virtual queue positions, and rebook dining without needing to coordinate with anyone else.

How to plan your day

Solo travellers should begin their Hollywood Studios day at rope drop, heading straight to Rise of the Resistance or Slinky Dog Dash before crowds build, then working through Millennium Falcon and Tower of Terror during the mid-morning lull. Use the hottest part of the afternoon for indoor experiences like Indiana Jones Epic Stunt Spectacular, air-conditioned shops along Hollywood Boulevard, or a leisurely table-service lunch, returning to high-demand rides in the cooler early evening hours. Finish your night in Galaxy's Edge as the area transforms after dark into one of the most atmospheric settings in any theme park, perfectly enjoyed at a solo traveller's own unhurried pace.
