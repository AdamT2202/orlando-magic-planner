EPCOT for Families with young kids in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect EPCOT trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting EPCOT with young kids in summer means diving into one of the busiest seasons at Walt Disney World, with large crowds especially on weekends and around Fourth of July. The Florida heat and humidity are intense from June through August, with temperatures regularly climbing above 90°F and frequent afternoon thunderstorms offering brief but heavy relief. Despite the busy atmosphere, EPCOT's wide open spaces, World Showcase lagoon, and family-friendly festivals create a magical and immersive environment that young children absolutely love.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Frozen Ever After – This gentle boat ride through the world of Arendelle is perfectly paced for toddlers and young children, featuring beloved characters and cool indoor air conditioning.
  • Remy's Ratatouille Adventure – A fun and not-too-scary ride that shrinks guests down to the size of a rat, delighting young imaginations with colorful scenes from the hit Pixar film.
  • Journey Into Imagination with Figment – Young kids love the playful purple dragon Figment, and the sensory surprises throughout this slow-moving ride keep little ones engaged and giggling.
  • EPCOT's World Showcase KidCino Passport – Kids can collect stamps from each country pavilion, turning a walk around the lagoon into an interactive adventure that sparks curiosity about world cultures.
  • Seas with Nemo and Friends – This clamshell-style ride through a virtual ocean alongside Nemo and friends is soothing, colorful, and ideal for even the youngest visitors, followed by a real aquarium to explore.

Dining tips

Young kids are well catered for at EPCOT, with quick-service options like Sunshine Seasons in The Land pavilion offering a wide variety of approachable dishes that even picky eaters will enjoy. For a sit-down experience, Coral Reef Restaurant provides a stunning underwater aquarium view that keeps children entertained throughout the meal, making it worth booking a table-service reservation well in advance. Snacking around World Showcase is a highlight for families, with kid-friendly bites like School Bread from Norway, frozen lemonade from America, and fresh fruit available throughout the pavilions. Be sure to bring refillable water bottles and take advantage of free ice water available at any quick-service location to keep little ones hydrated in the summer heat.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive at park opening – Get to EPCOT right when the gates open to enjoy the most popular rides like Frozen Ever After and Remy's Ratatouille Adventure before lines grow unmanageable in the midday heat.
  • Use Lightning Lane strategically – Purchase Lightning Lane selections for the highest-demand attractions early in the day so young kids spend less time waiting in the blazing sun.
  • Plan a midday break – Schedule a return to your hotel between noon and 3 PM for naps, a swim, and a chance to escape the peak heat and heaviest crowds of the day.
  • Pack rain gear – A compact poncho for each family member is essential, as summer afternoon storms arrive quickly and can last 30 to 60 minutes before clearing up completely.
  • Apply sunscreen frequently – Reapply SPF 50 sunscreen every two hours and use lightweight UV-protective clothing and hats to protect young children's sensitive skin throughout the day.

How to plan your day

Start your EPCOT day at World Discovery and World Nature as soon as the park opens, tackling high-demand rides like Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind and Frozen Ever After before the crowds build, then transition to slower-paced pavilions and World Showcase as the day progresses. Return to your resort for a midday rest during the hottest part of the afternoon, then head back to EPCOT in the early evening when temperatures drop, crowds thin slightly, and the World Showcase comes alive with entertainment and character meet-and-greets perfectly suited for young children. Ending the night watching EPCOT Forever, the park's spectacular nighttime lagoon show, gives families with young kids an unforgettable and low-effort finale to a well-planned summer day.
