Animal Kingdom for Families with young kids in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect Animal Kingdom trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Animal Kingdom with young children during the summer months of June through August means embracing a vibrant, energetic atmosphere filled with fellow families and enthusiastic park-goers. Crowd levels are at their peak during this period, so expect longer wait times at popular attractions, especially mid-morning through late afternoon. The Florida summer heat is intense, with temperatures frequently exceeding 90°F and afternoon thunderstorms that roll in almost daily, so preparation is essential to keeping little ones comfortable and happy throughout the day.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Festival of the Lion King – This colorful, high-energy live show features beloved characters, acrobatics, and sing-along moments that captivate toddlers and young children with minimal wait and guaranteed air-conditioned comfort.
  • Kilimanjaro Safaris – Young kids are amazed by real giraffes, elephants, and lions roaming freely, making this gentle open-air ride one of the most magical and accessible experiences in the park.
  • It's Tough to Be a Bug! – This fun 4D show inside the Tree of Life entertains young children with silly sensory surprises while giving families a cool, shaded break from the summer heat.
  • Wilderness Explorers – This interactive scavenger-style badge program keeps young children engaged throughout the entire park, encouraging exploration and learning at a relaxed, family-friendly pace.
  • Na'vi River Journey – This gentle boat ride through the bioluminescent world of Pandora is visually stunning and perfectly suited for young children who may not yet meet height requirements for thrill rides.

Dining tips

Flame Tree Barbecue is an excellent quick-service option for families, offering generous portions of kid-friendly smoked meats alongside outdoor shaded seating areas that catch a pleasant breeze near the water. For a sit-down experience, Tusker House Restaurant offers a character dining buffet with Donald Duck and friends, giving young children an unforgettable dining memory without chasing characters across a crowded park. Booking an early dining reservation at Tusker House not only secures your spot but also gets your family inside the park and seated before the summer heat reaches its daily peak. Always carry snacks and refillable water bottles to keep young children hydrated and energized between meals throughout the long summer day.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park gates 30 minutes before opening allows families with young children to experience the most popular attractions before crowds and heat build significantly.
  • Plan a midday break – Book a nearby hotel or use your resort to retreat between noon and 3 PM, when heat and afternoon storms are most intense and young children typically need rest.
  • Dress for the heat and rain – Light, moisture-wicking clothing, wide-brimmed hats, and compact ponchos are non-negotiable for keeping young kids comfortable through Florida's unpredictable summer weather.
  • Use the My Disney Experience app – Monitor Lightning Lane availability and live wait times in real time to make smart, efficient decisions that minimize time standing in the sun with young children.
  • Apply sunscreen frequently – Reapply SPF 50 or higher every 90 minutes, especially after water play areas or afternoon rain showers, to protect young children's sensitive skin throughout the day.

How to plan your day

Start your morning at rope drop by heading directly to Kilimanjaro Safaris and Na'vi River Journey, knocking out the two most popular family-friendly experiences before wait times climb past 30 minutes. Spend the late morning enjoying shows like Festival of the Lion King and It's Tough to Be a Bug!, which provide air-conditioned relief and require no ride height restrictions, keeping every member of your young family entertained. Return to the park in the early evening after a midday rest, when crowds thin slightly, the temperature drops, and Animal Kingdom's nighttime atmosphere creates a magical and memorable close to a perfect summer day.
