Hollywood Studios for Families with teens in Autumn

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in autumn offers families with teens a fantastic sweet spot between the busy summer season and the holiday rush, with noticeably lower crowd levels throughout September and much of October. The Florida heat begins to ease by November, making long days on your feet far more comfortable, though September still brings warm temperatures and the occasional afternoon thunderstorm. The park buzzes with a thrilling atmosphere, especially as Halloween and holiday overlays start appearing, giving teens plenty of immersive experiences to enjoy.

Top attractions for Families with teens

  • Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance – This jaw-dropping, fully immersive ride is a must for teens who love action, storytelling, and cutting-edge theme park technology.
  • Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run – Teens love the interactive co-pilot controls, making every ride a unique and competitive experience among friends and siblings.
  • Tower of Terror – A classic Hollywood Studios thrill that delivers genuine screams and an eerie storyline that older kids absolutely eat up.
  • Slinky Dog Dash – A family-friendly coaster that is surprisingly thrilling for all ages and perfectly paced for mixed-age groups visiting together.
  • Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring Aerosmith – Fast, loud, and set to a rock soundtrack, this inverting coaster is a guaranteed hit with thrill-seeking teens.

Dining tips

Teens tend to have big appetites after a day of riding, so consider booking a table at Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant, where vintage B-movie clips and car-shaped booths create a uniquely cool atmosphere that older kids genuinely appreciate. The Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo quick-service location in Galaxy's Edge offers creative, themed dishes that adventurous teen eaters will enjoy exploring. For a more flexible option, Fairfax Fare serves hearty portions of ribs, chicken, and loaded fries that satisfy hungry teenagers without a lengthy sit-down commitment. Booking advance dining reservations for table-service restaurants is strongly recommended in autumn, particularly around Halloween and Thanksgiving week when the park sees significant visitor spikes.

Autumn planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park gates before opening allows your family to experience Rise of the Resistance and other high-demand attractions before queues build significantly.
  • Purchase Lightning Lane passes strategically – Use individual Lightning Lane selections for the most popular rides like Rise of the Resistance to save considerable time during busier autumn weekends.
  • Pack layers and rain gear – September and October afternoons frequently bring short but heavy rain showers, so lightweight ponchos and a change of socks can save your day.
  • Check the entertainment schedule in advance – Autumn often brings special seasonal shows and limited-time experiences that teens will not want to miss, so plan your route around performance times.
  • Avoid Thanksgiving week if possible – The week of Thanksgiving is one of the busiest periods of the entire year at Hollywood Studios, with significantly longer wait times across all major attractions.

How to plan your day

Start your morning by heading directly to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge to experience Rise of the Resistance and Millennium Falcon before crowds gather, then work your way toward the thrill rides like Tower of Terror and Rock 'n' Roller Coaster through mid-morning. Use the hottest part of the early afternoon to grab a sit-down lunch, browse themed merchandise, or catch one of the park's indoor shows, giving everyone a comfortable break before the temperature begins to drop. As the evening approaches, return to any favourite attractions with shorter queues and finish the day at the Fantasmic nighttime spectacular, which delivers a memorable cinematic finale that teens and parents alike will talk about long after leaving Orlando.
