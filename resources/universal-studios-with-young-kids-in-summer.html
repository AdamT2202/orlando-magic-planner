Universal Studios Florida for Families with young kids in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect Universal Studios Florida trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Universal Studios Florida is a thrilling destination for families with young kids during the summer months of June through August, offering a magical mix of beloved characters, immersive lands, and kid-friendly rides. Expect large crowds, particularly on weekends and during the Fourth of July holiday week, as summer is one of the busiest periods of the year for the park. The Florida heat and humidity are intense during this season, with temperatures frequently reaching the low-to-mid 90s°F, but the park's water attractions and air-conditioned venues offer welcome relief throughout the day.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Despicable Me Minion Mayhem – A gentle 3D motion simulator ride that young children absolutely love, featuring their favourite Minion characters in a fun and not-too-intense adventure.
  • The Secret Life of Pets: Off the Leash! – A charming dark ride perfect for toddlers and early school-age kids, bringing the beloved movie characters to life in a colourful, low-scare environment.
  • Fievel's Playland – A giant outdoor play area inspired by An American Tail that gives young kids the freedom to climb, slide, and explore at their own pace.
  • E.T. Adventure – A classic and gentle bicycle ride through space that has delighted generations of young visitors, offering a sweet and imaginative experience ideal for little ones.
  • Animal Actors on Location! – A live show featuring trained animals that is educational, entertaining, and perfectly paced for young children who may not have the stamina for long queue lines.

Dining tips

Families with young kids will find plenty of convenient and kid-friendly dining options scattered throughout Universal Studios Florida, making it easy to refuel without losing too much park time. The Classic Monsters Café and Louie's Italian Restaurant offer familiar, crowd-pleasing meals like pizza, pasta, and rotisserie chicken that even the pickiest young eaters tend to enjoy. Bringing a small cooler bag with snacks, juice boxes, and fruit is a smart strategy during summer to keep energy levels up between meals and avoid meltdowns in the heat. Consider dining at off-peak hours, such as 11am or after 2pm, to avoid the longest restaurant wait times and secure a seat in air-conditioned comfort.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive at park opening – Gates typically open between 9am and 10am during summer, and arriving early allows your family to tackle the most popular young-kid attractions before lines build up significantly.
  • Pack sun protection essentials – Bring high-SPF sunscreen, UV-protective hats, sunglasses, and a lightweight stroller with a canopy to shield young children from the intense Florida sun throughout the day.
  • Schedule a midday break – The hours between 12pm and 3pm are the hottest and most crowded; consider returning to your hotel for a nap or swim and heading back to the park in the late afternoon when temperatures begin to drop.
  • Download the Universal Orlando app – Use the official app to monitor real-time wait times, locate character meet-and-greets, and find the nearest family restrooms and nursing facilities across the park.
  • Purchase tickets and parking in advance – Summer tickets sell at premium prices, so booking online ahead of your visit can save money and guarantee entry on your preferred date without stressful day-of queues.

How to plan your day

Begin your day by heading straight to the most popular young-kid attractions like Despicable Me Minion Mayhem and E.T. Adventure as soon as the park opens, when wait times are shortest and your children are at their most energetic. Build in regular hydration breaks, shaded rest stops, and a longer midday pause to keep young kids happy and prevent heat exhaustion during the peak afternoon hours. End your day on a high note by visiting character meet-and-greets, catching a live show like Animal Actors on Location, or exploring Fievel's Playland in the cooler early evening when the park atmosphere is at its most relaxed and enjoyable.
