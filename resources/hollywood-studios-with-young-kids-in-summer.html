Hollywood Studios for Families with young kids in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in summer is a buzzing, high-energy destination that draws large crowds, particularly families taking advantage of school holidays between June and August. Expect hot and humid Florida weather with afternoon thunderstorms that are brief but intense, so building flexibility into your schedule is essential. Despite the heat and busy atmosphere, the park offers a fantastic mix of immersive lands and family-friendly entertainment that makes it well worth the visit for parents with young children.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Toy Story Land – This colourful, pint-sized land is perfectly scaled for young children, with Slinky Dog Dash offering a gentle coaster experience that thrills without overwhelming little ones.
  • Alien Swirling Saucers – A fun, low-intensity spinning ride inside Toy Story Land that young kids absolutely love and that has minimal wait-time anxiety for nervous toddlers.
  • Muppet*Vision 3D – A hilarious and air-conditioned 3D show that gives the whole family a welcome cool-down while entertaining kids with beloved Muppet characters.
  • Disney Junior Play and Dance – Ideal for toddlers and pre-schoolers, this live show features popular Disney Junior characters and encourages kids to dance, sing, and interact.
  • Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge exploration – Even young Star Wars fans are captivated by the incredible theming and interactive elements of this immersive land, especially Dok-Ondar's Den and the Millennium Falcon exterior.

Dining tips

Hollywood Studios has several dining options well suited to families with young children, with Woody's Lunch Box in Toy Story Land being a favourite for its kid-friendly menu, fun theming, and quick-service convenience. Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant is a great choice for a sit-down meal, as children are enchanted by the drive-in movie concept and the dimly lit, exciting atmosphere keeps restless little ones engaged. For a quick and easy snack break, grab a Lunch Box Tart or a Totchos from Woody's Lunch Box to fuel up between attractions. Be sure to make dining reservations well in advance for table-service restaurants, as summer demand fills spots quickly.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive at rope drop – Getting to the park before opening allows your family to tackle the most popular rides before the heat and crowds peak mid-morning.
  • Schedule a midday break – Plan to leave the park or retreat to your resort between noon and 3pm when temperatures and crowd levels are at their highest, then return refreshed in the late afternoon.
  • Dress for the heat – Lightweight, breathable clothing, UV-protective hats, and reef-safe sunscreen are non-negotiable for a comfortable summer day with young children.
  • Use the Lightning Lane – Purchasing Lightning Lane passes for must-do attractions like Slinky Dog Dash can significantly reduce time spent standing in the blazing sun with impatient toddlers.
  • Pack rain gear – Lightweight ponchos are essential as afternoon storms roll in almost daily in summer; having them ready means you won't need to cut your day short or pay inflated prices in-park.

How to plan your day

Start your morning at Toy Story Land as soon as the park opens, riding Slinky Dog Dash and Alien Swirling Saucers before queues build up, then head to Muppet*Vision 3D for a mid-morning cool-down and a well-earned rest for little legs. After a midday break away from the park to nap, recharge, and escape the heat, return in the late afternoon to explore Galaxy's Edge, catch Disney Junior Play and Dance, and enjoy the park as temperatures begin to drop. Wrap up your evening with the spectacular nighttime show Fantasmic!, which is a genuinely magical experience for young children and a memorable way to close out a big summer day.
