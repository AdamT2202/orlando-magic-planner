Epic Universe for Families with young kids in Summer

Everything you need to plan the perfect Epic Universe trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Visiting Epic Universe with young kids during summer means immersing your family in one of Orlando's most ambitious theme parks at its busiest and most vibrant time of year. Crowd levels peak throughout June, July, and August, with wait times for popular attractions stretching well beyond 60 minutes during midday hours, so early arrival is essential. The Florida summer heat is intense, with temperatures regularly exceeding 90°F and frequent afternoon thunderstorms, but the park's immersive themed worlds and indoor attractions make it manageable and magical for little ones.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Celestial Park carousel and splash areas – gentle, visually stunning rides and water play zones keep toddlers and preschoolers happily entertained without overwhelming them.
  • How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk family rides – low-intensity dragon-themed adventures deliver big excitement with height requirements friendly to younger guests.
  • The Wizarding World of Harry Potter – Ministry of Magic – enchanting walk-through environments and age-appropriate interactive wand experiences captivate kids of all ages.
  • Super Nintendo World character meet-and-greets – beloved Mario and friends characters delight young children and create unforgettable photo moments.
  • Universal Monsters dark rides – slower-paced, story-driven dark ride experiences introduce mild thrills in a structured, reassuring format perfect for curious young kids.

Dining tips

Epic Universe offers a wide range of family-friendly dining options designed to satisfy picky young eaters alongside adventurous adults, with quick-service restaurants spread across every themed world to minimise walking time with tired little legs. Look for kid's meal options at locations like the themed eateries in Celestial Park and Isle of Berk, where familiar food choices such as pasta, sandwiches, and chicken tenders are readily available. Booking advance dining reservations for table-service restaurants is strongly recommended during summer, as popular spots fill quickly and walk-up waits can be lengthy during peak hours. Bringing a refillable water bottle and taking advantage of free ice water available at counter-service locations is a smart way to keep young children hydrated throughout the hot summer day.

Summer planning tips

  • Arrive before park opening – entering the gates 30 to 45 minutes early allows young families to experience the most popular attractions before crowds build and heat intensifies.
  • Use Lightning Lane passes strategically – purchasing Lightning Lane selections for the highest-demand rides saves significant time and reduces meltdowns from long queue waits with young children.
  • Plan a midday resort break – retreating to your hotel pool between roughly 12pm and 3pm avoids the hottest, most crowded part of the day and lets kids recharge for an evening return.
  • Pack sun protection and rain gear – sunscreen, hats, UV-protective clothing, and a compact poncho are non-negotiable summer essentials for a comfortable day with young kids.
  • Download the Universal Orlando app – real-time wait times, interactive maps, and dining reservations management help parents navigate the park efficiently with young children in tow.

How to plan your day

Start your Epic Universe day with a rope-drop strategy, heading directly to your children's most-anticipated land first thing in the morning when queues are at their shortest and energy levels are highest. Structure the afternoon around indoor, air-conditioned experiences, shows, and character encounters to ride out the peak heat and storm windows, then return to outdoor lands and key attractions as the evening crowd thins and temperatures drop. Ending the night with the park's signature evening entertainment and a final leisurely stroll through the glowing themed worlds gives young kids a memorable, magical close to an unforgettable summer day.
