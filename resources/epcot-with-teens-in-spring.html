EPCOT for Families with teens in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect EPCOT trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to Expect

Visiting EPCOT with teens in spring means a vibrant, energetic atmosphere packed with exciting festivals, innovative attractions, and plenty of immersive experiences that appeal to older kids and young adults. Crowd levels tend to rise during spring break weeks in March and April, so expect busier parks mid-week during school holiday periods, with slightly calmer days in early March and mid-May. The weather is generally warm and pleasant, with temperatures ranging from the mid-60s to mid-80s Fahrenheit, though afternoon thunderstorms can occasionally roll in, especially toward late May.

Top Attractions for Families with Teens

  • Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind – A thrilling reverse-launch roller coaster set to a killer soundtrack that teens will want to ride multiple times.
  • Test Track – Lets teens design their own concept car before putting it through a high-speed performance simulation, blending creativity with excitement.
  • Soarin' Around the World – A stunning hang-gliding simulation that wows older kids with breathtaking global scenery and immersive sensory effects.
  • Mission: SPACE – An intense centrifuge-based space simulation with an advanced option that genuinely challenges even thrill-seeking teens.
  • EPCOT International Flower and Garden Festival – Running through late May, this spring festival features outdoor kitchens, live concerts, and stunning topiaries that give teens plenty to explore and enjoy.

Dining Tips

EPCOT's World Showcase offers an unbeatable variety of international dining options that adventurous teens tend to love, from Japanese ramen in the Japan Pavilion to loaded nachos and margaritas at La Cava del Tequila in Mexico. For a quick and satisfying meal, the Flower and Garden Festival's outdoor festival kitchens serve seasonal small plates inspired by global flavors, making it easy to graze and sample as a family. Reservations are strongly recommended for sit-down restaurants like Space 220 and Teppan Edo, especially during busy spring break weeks, so book through the My Disney Experience app as early as 60 days in advance. Teens with larger appetites will appreciate the hearty portions at the Biergarten Restaurant in Germany or the flavors available at the Les Halles Boulangerie-Patisserie in the France Pavilion.

Spring Planning Tips

  • Arrive early or use Lightning Lane – Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind and Test Track attract long queues, so rope-drop these headliners or purchase Lightning Lane passes to save valuable time.
  • Check the Flower and Garden Festival schedule – The festival runs from early March through late May and includes free topiary displays along with ticketed concerts at the America Gardens Theatre worth booking in advance.
  • Dress in layers and pack rain gear – Spring mornings can be cool while afternoons heat up quickly, and brief afternoon showers are common, especially in April and May.
  • Download the My Disney Experience app – Use it to monitor real-time wait times, make dining reservations, and access your Lightning Lane selections throughout the day.
  • Plan around spring break crowds – If flexibility allows, visit in early March or after mid-April to avoid the peak spring break rush and enjoy shorter wait times across the park.

How to Plan Your Day

Start your morning at EPCOT's Future World area by tackling the most popular thrill rides like Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind and Mission: SPACE before crowds build up later in the morning. After lunch, shift your focus to the World Showcase, which opens at 11:00 AM, giving teens the chance to explore different country pavilions, sample international snacks from the festival kitchens, and discover unique shopping and entertainment. End your evening in World Showcase for the spectacular EPCOT Forever nighttime spectacular, which provides a magical and memorable finale to a full day of adventure for the whole family.
