SeaWorld Orlando for Families with young kids in Autumn

Everything you need to plan the perfect SeaWorld Orlando trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Autumn is one of the best times to visit SeaWorld Orlando with young children, as the sweltering summer heat begins to ease and crowd levels drop noticeably after Labor Day weekend. September through November brings a more comfortable atmosphere with mild temperatures and shorter queue times, making it far easier to navigate the park with little ones in tow. The park also introduces its beloved Howl-O-Scream event in October, though families with young kids will want to plan around this adult-focused evening event and focus on daytime visits.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Sesame Street Land: A dedicated area filled with gentle rides and beloved characters, perfectly designed for toddlers and preschoolers who love Elmo, Cookie Monster, and friends.
  • Antarctica: Empire of the Penguin: A family-friendly ride with both mild and wild ride options, plus a walk-through penguin habitat that young children absolutely adore.
  • Dolphin Nursery: A free viewing area where kids can watch playful dolphins up close, making for a memorable and educational experience without any height restrictions.
  • Sea of Shallows: A touch-pool experience where young children can gently interact with starfish and other marine life under guided supervision.
  • Shamu's Happy Harbor: A sprawling water play area and soft playground space ideal for burning off energy, particularly enjoyable during the cooler autumn mornings.

Dining tips

SeaWorld Orlando offers a variety of family-friendly dining options suited to young children, including kid's meals at Sharks Underwater Grill and casual counter-service spots like Voyager's Smokehouse where picky eaters will find familiar favourites. Consider purchasing the All-Day Dining Deal before your visit, which offers excellent value for families planning a full day at the park and removes the stress of budgeting for multiple meals. Autumn weather makes outdoor seating areas far more pleasant, so look for shaded picnic tables near Sesame Street Land for a relaxed mid-day break with younger children. Arriving at dining venues slightly before peak lunch hours, around 11am, helps families with strollers avoid the busiest queues and secure comfortable seating.

Autumn planning tips

  • Visit on weekdays: September and October weekdays see significantly lower attendance than weekends, meaning shorter waits and a calmer environment for young children who can become overwhelmed in large crowds.
  • Pack layers for the evening: Autumn temperatures in Orlando can dip into the low 60s Fahrenheit after sunset, so bring a light jacket or hoodie for children if you plan to stay into the early evening.
  • Arrive at rope drop: Getting to the park at opening time allows families with young kids to experience the most popular attractions like Sesame Street Land before afternoon crowds build.
  • Check the Howl-O-Scream schedule: If visiting in October, confirm whether the park closes early for Howl-O-Scream events on certain evenings, and plan your departure time accordingly to avoid exposure to scary theming.
  • Book tickets in advance online: Purchasing tickets ahead of your visit typically saves money and allows you to pre-select parking, reducing stress on the day and maximising time inside the park.

How to plan your day

Start your morning in Sesame Street Land as soon as the park opens, when young children are at their most energetic and the queues are at their shortest, then work your way towards the animal experiences like the Dolphin Nursery and penguin habitat in the mid-morning. Schedule lunch around 11am to 11:30am to beat the midday rush, followed by a quieter rest period during the early afternoon when young children may need a nap or a calm break in a shaded area. Wrap up your visit with the late-afternoon animal shows and an evening stroll through the park, taking advantage of the cooler autumn temperatures that make SeaWorld Orlando such a pleasure to explore with young families during this season.
