Hollywood Studios for Families with young kids in Spring

Everything you need to plan the perfect Hollywood Studios trip — rides, dining, crowd tips and more.

What to expect

Hollywood Studios in spring is a magical experience for families with young kids, offering warm and sunny Florida weather with temperatures typically ranging from the mid-60s to the mid-80s Fahrenheit. Crowds begin to pick up in March due to spring break season, peaking around Easter, so expect busier park conditions compared to winter months. The atmosphere is vibrant and energetic, with special character meet-and-greets and live entertainment adding extra excitement for little ones throughout the season.

Top attractions for Families with young kids

  • Toy Story Land – This colorful, immersive land is perfectly scaled for young children, featuring gentle rides like Slinky Dog Dash and Alien Swirling Saucers that are thrilling without being overwhelming.
  • Fantasmic! – This spectacular nighttime show combines beloved Disney characters, water effects, and dazzling lights, creating a memorable evening experience that captivates kids of all ages.
  • Disney Junior Dance Party – Young children absolutely love meeting their favorite Disney Junior characters and dancing along in this high-energy, interactive live show.
  • Muppet-Vision 3D – This funny and gentle 3D film experience is perfect for toddlers and young kids who enjoy laughs without intense scares or long wait times.
  • Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge – While some attractions skew older, young kids delight in exploring the immersive alien environment and meeting characters like Chewbacca up close.

Dining tips

Hollywood Studios offers several family-friendly dining options that cater well to the tastes and schedules of young children. Woody's Lunch Box in Toy Story Land is an ideal quick-service spot serving kid-friendly comfort foods like grilled cheese sandwiches and totchos, with outdoor seating that keeps little ones engaged in the themed surroundings. For a sit-down experience, Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Restaurant features a unique drive-in movie atmosphere that entertains children while parents enjoy a relaxed meal. Be sure to make advance dining reservations as early as possible for table-service restaurants, especially during the busy spring break period.

Spring planning tips

  • Arrive early – Plan to be at the park gates at least 30 minutes before official opening to take advantage of Early Theme Park Entry if staying at a Disney resort, helping you tackle popular rides before crowds build.
  • Book Lightning Lane selections in advance – Spring crowds make Lightning Lane passes essential for high-demand attractions, so purchase and select your times as early in the morning as possible.
  • Pack sun protection – Spring sunshine in Orlando can be intense, so bring sunscreen, hats, and light breathable clothing to keep young kids comfortable throughout the day.
  • Plan a midday break – Temperatures and crowds peak in the early afternoon, making it a smart strategy to return to your hotel for nap time and come back refreshed for the evening shows.
  • Check the park calendar – Special events and extended park hours often occur during spring break weeks, so review the official Disney calendar ahead of your visit to maximize your experience.

How to plan your day

Start your morning in Toy Story Land to ride Slinky Dog Dash and Alien Swirling Saucers before the crowds arrive, then head to Disney Junior Dance Party and Muppet-Vision 3D during the mid-morning when young kids are at their most energetic. After a midday break back at your resort to rest and recharge, return to the park in the late afternoon to explore Galaxy's Edge and enjoy a relaxed dinner before closing out the evening with the spectacular Fantasmic! nighttime show. Keeping your schedule flexible and building in downtime ensures young children stay happy and the whole family enjoys a stress-free spring day at Hollywood Studios.
